**Available:** publicly accessible within the verified scope.  
**Public Beta / Developer Preview:** test or development versions with limitations; not a blanket production guarantee.  
**Candidate:** a validation stage, not general availability certification.  
**In Development / Planned:** not a claim of an installed, fully working customer release.

Company and solution descriptions must not invent customer deployments, results, certifications or platform availability. Consult [Products](PRODUCTS.md) and [Solutions](SOLUTIONS.md) for the customer-facing descriptions.
