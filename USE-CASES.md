# Choose KING AI by Use Case

**Main:** https://www.kingai.work/  
**Business:** vip@kingai.work

## English

### I need to get a small task done quickly
Start with **KING AI Online Tools**: https://tools.kingai.work/

Use it for lightweight browser tasks, formatting, conversion, calculation, data/text utility and other fast workflows. If the work becomes recurring or document-heavy, the natural next step is **KINGAI Office**.

### I create documents, spreadsheets, presentations or PDFs every day
Explore **KINGAI Office**: https://office.kingai.work/

Best when productivity itself is the primary need. Add **KINGAI Security** when important files and devices need stronger protection, and explore **KINGAIBOT** when recurring work can benefit from controlled intelligent assistance.

### I manage servers or technical infrastructure
Explore **KINGAI OPS**: https://ops.kingai.work/

Best for clearer server, infrastructure and edge operations. **KINGAI Security** is the natural trust layer; **KINGAIBOT** can become relevant where repetitive operational workflows benefit from controlled assistance.

### I need customer support, lead capture and sales follow-up
Explore **KING AI Customer OS**: https://kefu.kingai.work/

Best when repeated customer questions, public knowledge, qualification and follow-up need one coherent relationship layer. It can connect naturally with Office and other KING AI products as the business relationship expands.

### I want an AI-native computer experience
Explore **KINGAI OS**: https://os.kingai.work/

Best when intelligence should feel closer to the computing environment itself. Office, Security and BOT represent natural adjacent experiences.

### I want AI to help complete longer digital work
Explore **KINGAIBOT** through https://www.kingai.work/

The product direction centers on outcome-oriented assistance, continuity and control rather than blind autonomy.

### I want a virtual world that remembers me
Explore **AI Life**: https://life.kingai.work/

Relationships, memory, home, career, economy, city, travel and long-term milestones create a second-life experience designed around continuity.

### I want a digital pet game with long-term attachment and progression
Explore **KINGAI Pet**: https://pet.kingai.work/

Best for companionship, care, collection, evolution, team building, world exploration and long-term character attachment.

### I am interested in persistent digital identity and characters
Explore **Digital Life**: https://digital.kingai.work/

This is the broader direction around identity, memory, character continuity and long-lived digital experiences.

### I need a custom business website or internal system
Explore **USDX TECH**: https://usdxtech.com/

USDX TECH is an independent related business surface for websites, internal systems, workflow modernization and practical AI implementation. It is not a core KING AI product.
