# Explore KING AI by Problem

KING AI is easier to evaluate when you start with a problem rather than a product name.

Choose the situation that sounds most familiar.

## “Our knowledge exists, but people cannot find or reuse it quickly.”

Explore **Enterprise Knowledge & Memory**.

Typical starting points:

- internal policies and SOPs;
- product and service knowledge;
- technical documentation;
- onboarding material;
- customer knowledge;
- research archives;
- recurring management information.

Useful outcomes may include faster lookup, more consistent answers, better continuity and less repeated explanation.

→ [Enterprise Solutions](business/ENTERPRISE-SOLUTIONS.md)  
→ [Start a Knowledge Pilot](business/START-A-PILOT.md)

## “We keep repeating the same work every day or every week.”

Explore **Mission & Workflow Intelligence**.

Good candidates include:

- recurring research;
- document preparation;
- reporting;
- information triage;
- follow-up;
- operations coordination;
- recurring monitoring;
- structured preparation before human review.

The goal is not automation for its own sake. The goal is to remove repetition while keeping the important decisions clear.

→ [What Makes an AI Workflow Worth Paying For?](intelligence/2026-08-14-what-makes-an-ai-workflow-worth-paying-for.md)  
→ [Start With One Workflow](intelligence/2026-08-13-start-with-one-workflow-ai-pilot.md)

## “We bought AI tools, but everyone still uses them differently.”

Explore **AI Workforce & Role Design**.

Instead of one general assistant for every task, define specialized roles around responsibility, knowledge, tools, review and measurable outcomes.

Potential roles include:

- research analyst;
- customer knowledge specialist;
- operations coordinator;
- sales intelligence role;
- content/research role;
- technical support role;
- internal knowledge role.

→ [One General AI Assistant Is Not an AI Workforce](intelligence/2026-08-14-one-general-assistant-is-not-an-ai-workforce.md)  
→ [AI Workforce Operating Model](docs/AI-WORKFORCE-OPERATING-MODEL.md)

## “We want AI to act, but we cannot give it unlimited authority.”

Explore **Controlled Action & Human Approval**.

A useful system can separate observing, analyzing, recommending, drafting and bounded execution from higher-impact actions that require approval.

This creates a practical path between “AI can do nothing” and “AI can do everything.”

→ [Human Approval Is a Production Feature](intelligence/2026-08-14-human-approval-is-a-production-feature.md)  
→ [Trust Center](docs/TRUST-CENTER.md)

## “Our customers keep asking the same questions.”

Explore **Website & App Intelligence**.

Potential outcomes:

- better product discovery;
- guided service selection;
- knowledge support;
- onboarding;
- self-service;
- qualified escalation to people;
- better continuity across customer journeys.

→ [Enterprise Solutions](business/ENTERPRISE-SOLUTIONS.md)

## “We spend too much time tracking markets, technology or competitors.”

Explore **Research & Intelligence**.

Potential workflows include:

- technology radar;
- public-source monitoring;
- competitor observation;
- industry intelligence;
- recurring management briefs;
- evidence organization;
- change detection.

→ [KING AI Intelligence](intelligence/INDEX.md)  
→ [Global Agent Technology Radar](docs/GLOBAL-AGENT-TECHNOLOGY-RADAR-2026.md)

## “We need stronger continuity across projects and teams.”

Explore **Persistent Intelligence**.

The goal is not to remember everything. It is to preserve the context that makes future work easier: decisions, project background, useful knowledge, open questions, recurring goals and previous outcomes.

→ [Beyond the Chatbot](intelligence/2026-08-13-persistent-intelligence-beyond-chatbots.md)  
→ [Memory, Continuity & Evolution](docs/MEMORY-EVOLUTION.md)

## “We need to prove value before committing to a larger AI program.”

Start with a focused Pilot.

A good Pilot has:

- one recurring workflow;
- one responsible owner;
- approved information sources;
- clear review points;
- a small number of measurable outcomes;
- a defined decision about what happens next.

→ [Start a KING AI Pilot](business/START-A-PILOT.md)

# 按问题寻找 KING AI 方案

与其先研究产品名称，不如先从你已经存在的问题开始。

- **知识很多，但找不到、复用慢** → 企业知识与记忆
- **每天都在重复同一种工作** → Mission / Workflow Intelligence
- **买了 AI，但每个人使用方式完全不同** → AI Workforce / 专业 AI 岗位
- **希望 AI 行动，但不能给无限权限** → 受控行动与人工审批
- **客户总是在重复问同样的问题** → Website / App Intelligence
- **市场、技术、竞争情报收集太耗时** → KING AI Intelligence
- **项目每次都重新解释背景** → Persistent Intelligence
- **想先证明 ROI 再扩大投入** → 一个工作流的 Pilot
