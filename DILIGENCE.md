# KING AI — Public Due Diligence Guide

**Platform:** https://www.kingai.work/  
**Strategic contact:** vip@kingai.work

This document gives investors, strategic partners and enterprise evaluators a practical way to review KING AI without exposing confidential production technology.

## 1. What is being built?

KING AI is a connected intelligent product family spanning productivity, customer workflows, infrastructure operations, security, AI-native computing, controlled intelligent action, global intelligence and persistent digital-life experiences.

The portfolio thesis is described in:
- `README.md`

## 2. Is every product equally mature?

No. Review `PUBLIC-STATUS.md` and `PUBLIC-ROADMAP.md`. KING AI deliberately distinguishes released capability from Production Candidate, In Development, Pre-Alpha and Roadmap states.

Breadth should not be interpreted as universal production maturity.

## 3. What should be validated first?

### Product
- Does the product solve a real problem independently?
- Is the user journey understandable without learning the whole ecosystem?
- Is there a reason to return?

### Commercial
- Is the paid value connected to an outcome?
- Can a pilot establish measurable value?
- Can the strongest products form recurring relationships?

### Distribution
- Can public tools, content, product experiences and partnerships reduce acquisition friction?
- Is cross-product discovery helpful rather than forced?

### Trust
- Are roadmap and released capability clearly separated?
- Are higher-risk actions governed?
- Are public and private information boundaries disciplined?

### Execution
- Is the team reducing duplication and conflicting authorities?
- Do releases have verification gates?
- Are products prioritized by evidence rather than product count?

## 4. Public evidence available today

Public evaluators can inspect:
- official product websites;
- the unified public repository;
- KINGAI Global Intelligence's public product repository;
- public methodology and source material;
- public status and release policy;
- trust and disclosure documentation;
- approved public demos and product surfaces.

A public commit alone is not proof that a customer-facing capability is released.

## 5. Information appropriate for confidential diligence

Where a serious financing, enterprise or strategic process justifies it, additional information may be shared under appropriate confidentiality and authorization, such as:
- verified operating metrics;
- financial information;
- customer/pilot evidence;
- detailed product roadmaps;
- scoped architecture discussion;
- security and operational controls;
- cost structure and unit economics;
- contractual or partnership material.

This public repository does not provide that confidential material.

## 6. Information intentionally not public

Not exposed publicly:
- proprietary algorithms;
- internal prompts or orchestration;
- memory structures;
- private APIs;
- credentials or keys;
- production database internals;
- infrastructure topology;
- private security controls;
- unpublished datasets;
- confidential customer, partner or financial information.

## 7. Commercial diligence questions

A useful investor discussion should ask:
1. Which product currently has the strongest repeat use?
2. Which workflow has the clearest willingness to pay?
3. Which products are acquisition surfaces versus monetization surfaces?
4. Which enterprise pilots have measurable success criteria?
5. Which infrastructure costs scale with usage?
6. Where does cross-product adoption happen naturally?
7. Which product areas should be deprioritized if retention is weak?
8. How portable is the system across model or infrastructure providers?
9. What must remain human-controlled as agent capability increases?
10. Which metrics will be disclosed after they are stable and verified?

## 8. Risk review

Important risks include:
- portfolio breadth ahead of product-market fit;
- product-name overlap;
- infrastructure and AI cost growth;
- automation risk;
- dependency on external providers;
- security and privacy obligations;
- commercialization before retention is proven;
- overstatement of roadmap capability.

A credible strategy should reduce these risks through prioritization, governance, provider flexibility, release gates and evidence-based expansion.

## 9. What would strengthen the investment case?

The strongest evidence would be:
- retained users in a focused product;
- clear paid conversion;
- repeat business workflow use;
- enterprise pilot-to-production conversion;
- improving gross-margin or delivery economics;
- partner-led distribution;
- natural cross-product expansion;
- trusted operation under increasing automation depth.

## 10. Integrity rule

KING AI does not use this public diligence material to imply unverified revenue, customers, financing, valuation, certifications, partnerships or market leadership.

The purpose of diligence is to make uncertainty easier to evaluate, not to hide it.
