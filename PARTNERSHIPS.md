## English

KING AI is open to relationships that create real product value rather than partnerships announced only for visibility.

### Product & workflow integration
For platforms, software providers and business systems where a KING AI product can improve an existing workflow or where complementary products can create a clearer user journey.

### Distribution & channel
For organizations that can help relevant users discover, adopt or deploy KING AI products through credible channels.

### Enterprise implementation & support
For businesses that need a more structured relationship around productivity, customer operations, security, infrastructure or intelligent workflows.

### Infrastructure, MSP & managed services
Relevant to KINGAI OPS, Security and broader operational relationships where ongoing infrastructure management and support matter.

### Device & OEM
Relevant to future AI-native computing, security and distribution opportunities where product maturity and platform fit support a real customer benefit.

### Creator, media & entertainment
Relevant to AI Life, KINGAI Pet and Digital Life where characters, stories, worlds or creator experiences can expand through authentic collaborations.

### Strategic co-development
For relationships where both parties contribute meaningful product, distribution or domain value. KING AI does not present speculative discussions as signed partnerships.

## What makes a good fit

A strong relationship usually has:

- a clear user or customer problem;
- a defined product or distribution benefit;
- realistic scope and ownership;
- measurable next steps;
- respect for privacy, security and governance;
- no requirement to expose private source or confidential internals.

## What to include when contacting us

Please include:

- organization or project name;
- what you want to achieve;
- which KING AI product is relevant, if known;
- target users or customers;
- proposed scope or relationship type;
- a practical next step.

Contact: **vip@kingai.work**

## Integrity

A conversation, introduction or evaluation is not a signed partnership. Public materials only describe verified relationships as completed relationships.
