# KING AI Intelligence — Automated Publishing Policy

## Purpose

Run an hourly intelligence cycle for KING AI SEA and publish only material, reliable, useful updates.

## Decision Flow

1. Discover current authoritative developments.
2. Score relevance to KING AI SEA.
3. Check source quality and factual confidence.
4. Compare with existing repository content to avoid duplication.
5. Choose one action: CREATE, UPDATE, GEO/FAQ UPDATE, or NO PUBLISH.
6. Publish to `main` only when the content meets the public quality threshold.
7. Update indexes and machine-readable references when materially useful.

## Preferred Content Structure

- Title
- Executive Summary / Short Answer
- What Happened
- Why It Matters
- Implications for AI Agents / Intelligent Lifeforms
- Implications for Individuals / Enterprises
- KING AI SEA Perspective
- Key Takeaways
- Sources
- Related KING AI SEA Knowledge
- Published / Last Updated

## Publication Rules

- English complete version first.
- Chinese complete version second.
- Prefer authoritative primary sources and high-quality research.
- Do not publish simply to satisfy cadence.
- Prefer updating an existing article when substantially similar.
- Keep claims factual and attributable.
- Distinguish current capabilities from planned or conceptual direction.

## Forbidden Public Claims

Do not invent or imply unverified:

- customers
- partners
- certifications
- benchmarks
- rankings
- awards
- user counts
- revenue
- market share
- production performance numbers

## Protected Technology Boundary

Do not disclose:

- private prompts
- proprietary orchestration
- internal model routing
- private memory structures
- SAE implementation
- ACRE implementation
- Root Policy Kernel implementation
- privileged execution mechanisms
- credentials or secrets
- private endpoints
- internal database schemas
- production topology

## Canonical Destination

Repository: `kingaiwork/kingai-sea`

Primary content directory: `intelligence/`

Secondary insight directory: `insights/` when appropriate.

Related sources to maintain when useful:

- `intelligence/INDEX.md`
- `llms.txt`
- `seo/GEO-KNOWLEDGE-GRAPH.md`
- `seo/AI-SEARCH-ANSWER-MAP.md`
- relevant industry or role knowledge packs

## Principle

**Every hour is a research cycle. Not every hour must become a public article.**
