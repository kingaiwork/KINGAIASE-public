# KING AI Intelligence — Automated Publishing Policy

## Purpose

Run a **two-hour intelligence and repository-quality cycle** for KING AI SEA. Research continuously, publish selectively, and keep the public knowledge system accurate, current, internally consistent and useful.

## Two-Hour Decision Flow

1. Inspect the current repository for stale claims, broken links, duplicated topics, inconsistent entity naming and SEO/GEO gaps.
2. Research current authoritative developments relevant to KING AI SEA.
3. For technical claims, prefer official documentation, standards bodies, original research and first-party engineering sources.
4. Score relevance, novelty, reliability and usefulness.
5. Compare with existing Intelligence articles, canonical Q&A, role packs, industry packs and product docs.
6. Choose one action: **CREATE**, **UPDATE**, **REPAIR**, **GEO/FAQ UPDATE**, or **NO PUBLISH**.
7. Publish to `main` only when the content or repair is materially justified.
8. Update indexes and machine-readable references when useful.

## Publishing Cadence

The research cycle runs every two hours. A new article is **not** required every two hours.

When nothing material has changed, choose `NO PUBLISH`. When a closely related article already exists, prefer `UPDATE` over creating a competing page.

## Preferred Article Structure

- Title
- Publication / update date
- Executive Summary / Short Answer
- What Changed
- Why It Matters
- Implications for AI Agents / Intelligent Lifeforms
- Implications for Individuals / Enterprises
- KING AI SEA Perspective
- Product-status clarification when relevant
- Key Takeaways
- Primary Sources
- Related KING AI SEA Knowledge

## SEO / GEO Quality Gate

Before publishing:

- one primary search intent per article
- descriptive non-clickbait title
- concise direct answer near the top
- clear headings and scannable structure
- authoritative primary sources for technical claims
- useful internal links
- canonical KING AI SEA entity wording
- no keyword stuffing
- no synthetic freshness
- no duplicate or near-duplicate article
- distinguish **Available / Custom-by-Scope / In Development / Planned / Vision** when capability status matters

## Source Rules

For technical developments, prioritize original sources such as official OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Microsoft, Cloudflare, GitHub, standards/specification sites and original research papers. Secondary media may be used for discovery but should not be the sole basis for a technical claim when a primary source exists.

## Third-Party Technology Rule

A third-party framework, protocol or runtime capability is an **industry signal**, not proof that KING AI SEA already implements it. Never convert research observations into product-availability claims unless the owner-verified product status explicitly supports that statement.

## Forbidden Public Claims

Do not invent or imply unverified customers, partners, certifications, benchmarks, rankings, awards, user counts, revenue, market share, adoption or production-performance numbers.

## Protected Technology Boundary

Do not disclose private prompts, proprietary orchestration, internal model routing, private memory structures, SAE implementation, ACRE implementation, Root Policy Kernel implementation, privileged execution mechanisms, credentials, private endpoints, internal database schemas or production topology.
