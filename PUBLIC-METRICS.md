# KING AI — Public Metrics Framework

KING AI prefers measurable progress over inflated storytelling. This document defines the metrics that matter as products mature. It is a framework, not a claim that every metric is currently published.

## Company-level metrics

### Product adoption
- activated users by product;
- repeat users;
- retained users by cohort;
- frequency of meaningful use;
- cross-product adoption where the next product solves a real adjacent need.

### Commercial quality
- free-to-paid conversion where applicable;
- recurring revenue by product category when approved for disclosure;
- qualified business opportunities;
- pilot-to-production conversion;
- expansion from initial scope to additional workflows;
- support and implementation efficiency.

### Distribution
- organic/search acquisition;
- direct traffic and repeat visits;
- product-led referrals;
- partner-sourced adoption;
- content-to-product conversion;
- acquisition cost only when measured reliably.

### Trust and reliability
- uptime and successful release rate;
- issue resolution time;
- rollback/recovery success;
- security incident handling;
- false-positive or failure rates where applicable;
- customer support response and resolution quality.

## Product-specific metrics

### Online Tools
- completed useful tasks;
- repeat visits;
- search-to-tool conversion;
- natural expansion into another product.

### Office
- active editing sessions;
- documents/workflows completed;
- retention;
- compatibility success;
- recovery success;
- premium/team adoption when available.

### Security
- successful scans/protection actions;
- false-positive rate;
- quarantine/recovery outcomes;
- update health;
- retained protected devices when publicly reportable.

### OPS
- managed environments;
- successful governed operations;
- incident or task resolution time;
- automation completion and rollback quality;
- operator time saved.

### Customer OS
- supported conversations;
- known-question resolution;
- human handoff quality;
- qualified leads;
- follow-up completion;
- customer knowledge freshness.

### SEA / KINGAIBOT
- successful multi-step workflow completion;
- approval/escalation rate;
- human takeover success;
- retry/recovery quality;
- recurring workflow retention;
- value per completed workflow rather than message volume alone.

### Global Intelligence
- returning professional users;
- country comparison usage;
- watchlist/monitoring engagement when released;
- methodology/source usage;
- data-delivery demand;
- research or institutional conversion where verified.

### AI Life / Pet / Digital Life
- day/week/month retention;
- return frequency;
- progression depth;
- relationship/content engagement;
- paid content conversion when available;
- creator or expansion engagement where released.

## Investor KPI hierarchy

The most important order is:

**Usefulness → Retention → Paid value → Efficient distribution → Expansion → Defensibility.**

Vanity metrics should not substitute for this sequence.

## Disclosure rules

Numbers should be published only when:
- they are measured from a defined source;
- the time window is stated;
- the metric definition is stable enough to interpret;
- internal/private information is not exposed;
- the number has been approved for public release.

Do not publish estimated users, ARR, market share, savings or performance as facts merely because they sound attractive.

## Why this matters

A serious investor should be able to see how KING AI intends to prove product-market fit over time. A serious customer should be able to ask what success looks like before buying. This framework provides that shared language without manufacturing traction.
