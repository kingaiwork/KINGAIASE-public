## Investor summary

The investment thesis is not that one AI app replaces all software. It is that AI-native products can create stronger economics when different surfaces create different reasons to return:

- discovery through utilities and public products;
- frequency through productivity, customer and operations workflows;
- trust through security and computing environments;
- value per workflow through controlled action;
- long-duration engagement through persistent digital life;
- professional depth through global intelligence and data workflows.

The portfolio should be evaluated on product usefulness, retention, commercial conversion, trust, distribution and disciplined execution—not on the number of product names.

## Messaging rules

Use:

- persistent intelligence;
- governed AI workforce;
- approved context;
- controlled action;
- human authority;
- explicit permissions;
- long-term continuity;
- product-specific value;
- verified public status;
- roadmap when discussing future work.

Avoid unsupported language such as:

- conscious AI;
- fully autonomous company;
- zero-risk security;
- replaces all employees;
- guaranteed outcomes;
- market leader;
- millions of users;
- enterprise customers or partnerships that have not been publicly verified;
- capabilities that remain in development.

## Public/private boundary

Safe to discuss publicly:

- user problems;
- product benefits;
- public UX;
- published status;
- commercial models;
- use cases;
- methodology;
- high-level governance principles;
- official product links.

Keep private:

- proprietary algorithms;
- internal prompts;
- memory structure;
- model routing;
- credentials;
- private APIs;
- production topology;
- database internals;
- security implementation;
- unpublished operational data;
- confidential customer information.
