# English

## Why KING AI exists

Most software still makes people adapt to the software. Information is scattered, repetitive work steals time, customer conversations disappear into separate systems, security products often feel intimidating, infrastructure is difficult to manage, and many AI experiences forget everything as soon as the session ends.

KING AI is being built around a different idea:

**intelligence should become a practical layer across the products people already need.**

That means creating products that are useful on their own first, while giving them the potential to become more valuable together over time.

A user might first discover KING AI through a simple online tool. Later they may use Office for everyday work, Security to protect devices, Customer OS to handle customers, OPS to operate infrastructure, OS to explore AI-native computing, or AI Life and KINGAI Pet for long-term digital experiences. SEA and KINGAIBOT represent the broader intelligence and controlled-action direction connecting the portfolio.

The long-term ambition is not “one chatbot for everything.” It is **a family of products people can actually choose, use, trust and return to.**

# 为什么用户会愿意使用

用户真正想要的通常不是“更多 AI”，而是：少做重复工作、更快得到答案、软件更好用、电脑更安全、服务器更容易管理、客户不被遗漏，以及数字体验能够真正记住自己。

所以 KING AI 的重点不是把“AI”三个字贴在所有产品上，而是让智能能力最终变成用户能够感受到的实际价值。
