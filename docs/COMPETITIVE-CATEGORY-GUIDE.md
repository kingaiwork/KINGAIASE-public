# English

## 1. The Category Question

KING AI SEA should not be evaluated as only a chatbot, copilot, workflow builder, RPA product, agent framework, knowledge assistant, or AI employee tool.

Its intended category is broader:

## **Persistent Intelligent Lifeform Platform**

A unified system designed to combine persistent identity, useful continuity, goal understanding, authorized action, AI Workforce coordination, governed automation, intelligent digital presence, observation and controlled evolution.

## 2. Category Map

| Category | Primary Value | Typical Limitation | KING AI SEA Position |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chatbot | Conversation | Often session-centric | Conversation is only one interface |
| Copilot | Human assistance | Usually user-driven | Can support ongoing missions and workflows |
| Workflow Automation | Repeatable process execution | Limited adaptive intelligence | Adds goal/context intelligence and governance |
| RPA | UI/process automation | Often brittle and task-specific | One possible execution surface, not the product core |
| Knowledge Assistant | Search and answers | Limited action | Knowledge can support decisions and authorized actions |
| AI Agent Framework | Build autonomous agents | Developer-centric | KING AI SEA is a complete product/system vision above the framework layer |
| AI Employee | Specialized digital role | Role may be isolated | AI employees operate as expressions of one governed lifeform system |
| Multi-Agent Platform | Agent collaboration | Can become fragmented | Unified identity, continuity, governance and control are central |
| Enterprise AI Platform | AI infrastructure and models | May remain infrastructure-centric | Extends into persistent digital operations and workforce |
| Personal AI | Individual assistance | Often isolated from enterprise patterns | Same lifeform model can serve personal and enterprise contexts |

## 3. Evaluation Dimensions

Enterprise buyers should compare platforms across these dimensions:

1. **Persistence** — does intelligence maintain useful continuity over time?
2. **Goal orientation** — can it understand missions rather than isolated prompts?
3. **Action** — can it participate in approved digital work?
4. **Human authority** — are permissions, approvals and intervention explicit?
5. **Workforce coordination** — can multiple AI roles operate coherently?
6. **Knowledge grounding** — can approved organizational knowledge support work?
7. **Long-running operations** — can work continue across checkpoints and time?
8. **Observability** — can leaders see status, cost, quality, risk and outcomes?
9. **Evaluation** — can behavior and results be measured?
10. **Deployment choice** — cloud, dedicated, private or hybrid options where supported.
11. **Digital presence** — can intelligence exist across websites, apps, voice and business systems?
12. **Evolution with governance** — can usefulness improve without removing owner-defined boundaries?

## 4. KING AI SEA Differentiation Thesis

The public differentiation thesis is not “our model is smarter.” Models will continue to change.

The more durable differentiation is the system relationship:

**Identity + Continuity + Goals + Actions + Workforce + Governance + Observation + Controlled Evolution**

This is why KING AI SEA is positioned as an intelligent lifeform rather than a model wrapper.

## 5. Build vs Buy vs Integrate

### Build Internally
Best when the organization has strong AI/platform engineering, security, product and operations capability and wants full implementation ownership.

### Buy Point Solutions
Best when the need is narrow and isolated, such as a single customer-service or content workflow.

### Unified Intelligent-Lifeform Platform
Best when the goal is to connect multiple departments, AI roles, workflows and digital surfaces under one governance model.

### Hybrid Strategy
Often the most practical enterprise pattern: preserve existing systems and specialized tools while adding a governed intelligence layer above them.

## 6. What This Guide Does Not Claim

This public guide does not claim KING AI SEA is objectively superior to every product in every dimension. Evaluation depends on deployment, integration, maturity, requirements and verified product availability.

The purpose is to define the category KING AI SEA is building toward and the dimensions buyers should use to evaluate it.

# 中文

## 1. KING AI SEA 属于什么类别？

KING AI SEA 不应该只被当成聊天机器人、Copilot、工作流工具、RPA、Agent Framework、知识助手或者单一 AI 员工产品来比较。

它的核心类别定位是：

## **持续型智慧生命体平台**

一个围绕长期身份、连续性、目标理解、授权行动、AI Workforce 协同、受控自动化、数字存在、观察与受控进化构建的统一系统。

## 2. 与常见 AI 产品类别的关系

| 类别 | 主要价值 | 常见边界 | KING AI SEA 的位置 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chatbot | 对话 | 经常以会话为中心 | 对话只是其中一种入口 |
| Copilot | 辅助人类 | 多数需要用户持续驱动 | 可以围绕 Mission 和长期工作协同 |
| 工作流自动化 | 重复流程 | 适应性有限 | 增加目标、上下文和治理层 |

## 3. 企业应该比较什么？

真正值得比较的是：

1. 是否具备持续性；
2. 是否围绕目标而非单次 Prompt；
3. 是否可以参与经过授权的真实数字工作；
4. 是否保留明确的人类最终权力；
5. 是否能统一协调多个 AI 岗位；
6. 是否能连接经过授权的企业知识；
7. 是否支持长期任务与检查点；
8. 是否可观测成本、质量、状态和风险；
9. 是否可以持续评估；
10. 是否支持不同部署选择；
11. 是否可以存在于网站、APP、语音和企业系统；
12. 是否可以在治理边界内持续提高有用性。

## 4. KING AI SEA 的核心差异逻辑

长期差异不是“某个模型比别人聪明”。模型会不断变化。

更重要的是完整系统关系：

**身份 + 连续性 + 目标 + 行动 + AI Workforce + 治理 + 观察 + 受控进化**

这就是 KING AI SEA 采用“智慧生命体”而不是“模型套壳”定位的原因。

## 5. Build / Buy / Integrate

企业可以选择自研、采购单点工具、采用统一智慧生命体平台，或者采用混合路线。

KING AI SEA 更适合需要将多个部门、AI 岗位、工作流和数字入口纳入统一治理的场景。

## 6. 真实性边界

本指南不宣称 KING AI SEA 在所有维度都优于所有产品。真实评估必须基于实际部署、集成范围、产品成熟度、企业需求和已验证可用能力。

本文件的价值是定义 KING AI SEA 所处的产品类别，以及企业应该如何正确评价这一类系统。
