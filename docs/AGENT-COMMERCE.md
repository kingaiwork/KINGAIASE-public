# English

## From Conversation to Commercial Action

As AI systems become capable of assisting with discovery, comparison, booking, purchasing, procurement, billing and fulfillment, commercial workflows can increasingly become agent-assisted.

KING AI SEA can support governed commerce experiences while keeping authorization, payment infrastructure, business rules and human approval under operator control.

## Consumer & Service Commerce

Potential experiences include:

- product or service discovery
- recommendation assistance
- comparison
- quote requests
- appointment booking
- reservation workflows
- subscription selection
- order-status assistance
- return and support workflows
- renewal reminders

## Enterprise Procurement

Potential business workflows include:

- purchase request preparation
- vendor information gathering
- quote comparison
- approval routing
- procurement status tracking
- invoice coordination
- renewal management
- supplier communication assistance

## AI Sales Agent + Commerce

An AI sales employee can help move a customer from question to qualified next step:

**Discover → Understand Need → Recommend → Qualify → Quote/Book → Approve → Handoff/Complete → Follow Up**

## Website Commerce Agent

A public website agent can combine conversation, product knowledge and business workflows so visitors can receive guided assistance without navigating every page manually.

## Payment Governance

Financial actions should use appropriate external payment infrastructure and explicit authorization. Depending on the use case, controls may include:

- confirmation before payment
- transaction limits
- business approval
- role-based permissions
- audit records
- merchant/payment-provider controls
- fraud and risk controls
- human escalation

## Agent-to-Agent Commerce

In a future agent ecosystem, a buyer’s agent may communicate with a merchant or service agent to discover availability, compare options, prepare an order or booking, and present the final action for authorization.

KING AI SEA can be designed to participate in these emerging agent-commerce environments while maintaining owner-defined policy and governance.

## Business Outcome

The objective is not autonomous spending without oversight.

The objective is to reduce friction from **intent → qualified commercial action** while keeping control with the user or organization.

# 中文

## 从对话进一步走向商业行动

随着 AI 能够参与发现、比较、预约、购买、采购、账单和履约等流程，越来越多商业流程会进入 Agent-assisted Commerce 阶段。

KING AI SEA 可以支持受治理的智能体商业体验，同时把授权、支付基础设施、业务规则和人工审批掌握在用户或企业手中。

## 面向消费者和服务行业

可以支持：

- 产品/服务发现
- 推荐辅助
- 对比
- 询价
- 预约
- 预订
- 订阅选择
- 订单状态
- 退换/客服流程
- 续费提醒

## 企业采购

可以支持：

- 采购请求准备
- 供应商资料收集
- 报价比较
- 审批流转
- 采购状态跟踪
- 发票协调
- 续约管理
- 供应商沟通辅助

## AI 销售员工 + Commerce

AI 销售员工可以帮助客户从一个问题逐步走到真正的商业下一步：

**发现 → 理解需求 → 推荐 → 判断 → 报价/预约 → 审批 → 交接/完成 → 后续跟进**

## 网站 Commerce Agent

官网上的智慧 Agent 可以把对话、企业产品知识与后台业务流程连接起来，让访客不必自己浏览所有页面，也能获得引导式商业服务。

## 支付治理

涉及资金的操作应使用适当的外部支付基础设施，并要求明确授权。根据不同业务场景可以设置：

- 支付前确认
- 金额限制
- 企业审批
- 角色权限
- 审计记录
- 商户/支付平台控制
- 风险控制
- 人工升级

## Agent-to-Agent Commerce

未来买方 Agent 可以与商家或服务 Agent 沟通，查询可用性、比较方案、准备订单或预订，并把最终动作交给用户授权。

KING AI SEA 可以面向这种未来智能体商业网络进行兼容，同时保持用户定义的策略和治理。

## 最终商业价值

目标不是让 AI 在无人监管下自由花钱。

目标是减少 **用户意图 → 合格商业行动** 之间的摩擦，同时让最终控制权始终掌握在个人或企业手中。
