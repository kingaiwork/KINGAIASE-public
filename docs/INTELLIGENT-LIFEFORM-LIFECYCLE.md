# English

## 1. From Activation to Persistent Intelligence

KING AI SEA is designed around continuity rather than one-time interaction.

The public lifecycle can be understood as eight stages:

```text
Activate → Understand → Connect → Assist → Operate → Observe → Adapt → Evolve
```

This is a product-level lifecycle, not an internal algorithm.

## 2. Stage One — Activate

The intelligent lifeform is introduced to its owner, organization, role and operating boundaries.

Public configuration may include:

- owner or organization
- mission
- identity
- role
- language
- communication channels
- approved systems
- permission boundaries
- deployment environment

## 3. Stage Two — Understand

KING AI SEA begins building a useful understanding of:

- goals
- priorities
- projects
- responsibilities
- terminology
- business context
- preferred working style
- important constraints

The goal is not merely to remember words, but to preserve meaningful working context.

## 4. Stage Three — Connect

The intelligent lifeform may connect to approved knowledge and systems such as:

- documents
- email
- calendar
- CRM
- ERP
- websites
- applications
- APIs
- cloud services
- databases
- developer tools
- communication systems

Connections remain subject to the owner's permissions and environment policies.

## 5. Stage Four — Assist

At the assistance stage, KING AI SEA supports work while humans remain directly in the loop.

Examples:

- research
- summarization
- drafting
- planning
- analysis
- recommendation
- knowledge retrieval
- report preparation
- task preparation

## 6. Stage Five — Operate

As trust, integration and governance mature, approved workflows can move from recommendation toward execution.

Examples:

- follow-up
- task routing
- content publishing workflows
- system checks
- customer-service operations
- recurring reporting
- approved data updates
- workflow coordination

Execution authority remains defined by the owner or organization.

## 7. Stage Six — Observe

A persistent intelligent lifeform should be able to understand the outcome of its work.

Public observation may include:

- task status
- customer response
- workflow completion
- system state
- operational signals
- exceptions
- quality feedback
- business metrics

## 8. Stage Seven — Adapt

Based on approved context and feedback, KING AI SEA can become better aligned with:

- working style
- preferred outputs
- recurring patterns
- business priorities
- escalation preferences
- role expectations

Adaptation is not presented as unrestricted self-modification. It is an owner-governed improvement relationship.

## 9. Stage Eight — Evolve

Within the unified KING AI SEA system, **Evolution OS** and **SAE** represent the public concepts for controlled evolution.

A high-level public loop is:

```text
Sense → Reflect → Propose → Validate → Evolve
```

The proprietary algorithms, validation logic, thresholds and implementation remain private.

## 10. Lifecycle Across Time

### Day 1
Understands identity, role, boundaries and initial goals.

### Week 1
Builds useful project and workflow context.

### Month 1
Recognizes recurring work, preferences and operational patterns.

### Quarter 1+
Can become part of the owner's or organization's operating rhythm, subject to governance and actual deployment capabilities.

## 11. Lifecycle for Enterprises

For an organization, lifecycle maturity may progress through:

1. one AI role
2. connected knowledge
3. approved business-system integrations
4. repeatable workflows
5. multiple AI employees
6. AI Workforce coordination
7. unified Control Center
8. cross-department persistent intelligence

## 12. Lifecycle Principle

### **The relationship grows. The authority remains governed.**

# 中文

## 1. 从激活到长期智慧

KING AI SEA 的核心不是“一次回答”，而是“长期连续关系”。

公开生命周期可以理解为八个阶段：

```text
激活 → 理解 → 连接 → 协助 → 运营 → 观察 → 适应 → 进化
```

这是对外产品生命周期，不是内部算法说明。

## 2. 第一阶段 — 激活 Activate

智慧生命体首先建立自己的主人、企业、角色和运行边界。

公开配置可能包括：

- 所有者或组织
- Mission
- 身份
- 角色
- 语言
- 沟通渠道
- 授权系统
- 权限边界
- 部署环境

## 3. 第二阶段 — 理解 Understand

KING AI SEA 逐步建立对以下内容的有用理解：

- 目标
- 优先级
- 项目
- 职责
- 企业术语
- 业务上下文
- 工作偏好
- 重要限制条件

目标不是简单“记住文字”，而是形成有意义的工作连续性。

## 4. 第三阶段 — 连接 Connect

智慧生命体可以连接经过授权的知识和系统，例如：

- 文档
- Email
- Calendar
- CRM
- ERP
- 网站
- APP
- API
- 云服务
- 数据库
- 开发工具
- 沟通系统

所有连接都继续受使用者权限和环境策略控制。

## 5. 第四阶段 — 协助 Assist

在协助阶段，人类仍然直接参与主要决策，KING AI SEA 负责：

- 研究
- 总结
- 草拟
- 规划
- 分析
- 建议
- 知识检索
- 报告准备
- 任务准备

## 6. 第五阶段 — 运营 Operate

随着信任、集成和治理成熟，部分经过授权的工作流可以从“建议”进一步进入“执行”。

例如：

- 自动跟进
- 任务分流
- 内容发布工作流
- 系统检查
- 客户服务
- 周期性报表
- 授权数据更新
- 工作流协调

执行权始终由所有者或企业定义。

## 7. 第六阶段 — 观察 Observe

长期智慧生命体不仅要执行，也要理解执行之后发生了什么。

公开观察可以包括：

- 任务状态
- 客户反馈
- 工作流完成情况
- 系统状态
- 运营信号
- 异常
- 质量反馈
- 业务指标

## 8. 第七阶段 — 适应 Adapt

通过经过授权的上下文和反馈，KING AI SEA 可以逐渐更符合：

- 工作方式
- 输出偏好
- 重复模式
- 企业优先级
- 升级偏好
- 岗位要求

这种适应不是无限制自我修改，而是由所有者治理的长期优化关系。

## 9. 第八阶段 — 进化 Evolve

在统一 KING AI SEA 内部，**Evolution OS** 和 **SAE** 用来表达受控进化理念。

公开高层循环为：

```text
感知 Sense → 反思 Reflect → 提议 Propose → 验证 Validate → 进化 Evolve
```

具体算法、验证逻辑、阈值与实现保持私有。

## 10. 时间维度上的生命周期

### 第一天
理解身份、职责、边界和初始目标。

### 第一周
建立有价值的项目和工作流上下文。

### 第一个月
开始识别重复工作、偏好和运营模式。

### 第一季度及以后
在实际部署能力和治理允许范围内，逐渐成为个人或企业运营节奏的一部分。

## 11. 企业生命周期

企业成熟路径可以从：

1. 一个 AI 角色
2. 企业知识接入
3. 授权业务系统连接
4. 可重复工作流
5. 多个 AI 员工
6. AI Workforce 协同
7. 统一 Control Center
8. 跨部门长期企业智慧

逐步发展。

## 12. 生命周期原则

### **关系可以不断成长，最终权力始终受治理。**
