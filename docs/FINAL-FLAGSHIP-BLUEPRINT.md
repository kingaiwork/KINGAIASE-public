**KING AI SEA** is a unified persistent intelligent-lifeform system for individuals, organizations, developers and digital operations. It is designed to understand goals, preserve useful continuity, coordinate specialized AI roles, connect approved systems, assist with or execute authorized digital work, observe outcomes, and improve over time under owner-defined boundaries.

KING AI SEA is the mother system. AI Workforce, AI Employees, Personal AI, Website AI, App AI, industry intelligence, automation, developer ecosystem and Agent Commerce are expressions or extensions of the same unified intelligent lifeform.

The public capability model is:

**Intelligence → Identity → Continuity → Mission → Action → Observation → Learning → Controlled Evolution → Governance**

Public concepts inside KING AI SEA:

- **Evolution OS** — operating architecture concept.
- **SAE** — controlled self-evolution mechanism concept.
- **ACRE** — active-defense / immune-system security concept.
- **Root Policy Kernel** — high-authority policy and execution-boundary concept.

Their proprietary implementation is not published.
