# 🏛️ KING AI SEA — Enterprise Operating Governance

# English

## Purpose
Define how a persistent intelligent lifeform, AI Workforce and human organization operate together without losing accountability.

## Governance Bodies
- Executive Sponsor — owns business outcome and risk appetite.
- AI Governance Owner — owns policy, scope and cross-functional governance.
- Mission Owner — accountable for a specific business mission.
- System Owner — accountable for connected systems and data.
- Security Owner — reviews sensitive permissions and incident controls.
- Human Approver — approves defined high-impact actions.
- Operations Owner — monitors production service, quality and continuity.

## Governance Objects
Every production capability should have:
1. named mission
2. business owner
3. AI role(s)
4. data scope
5. tool scope
6. action scope
7. approval rules
8. escalation path
9. KPI / quality targets
10. deployment environment
11. logging / evidence expectations
12. review cadence

## Decision Rights
**Humans retain authority over:** strategy, legal commitments, employment decisions, regulated judgments, high-risk financial actions, irreversible/destructive operations, security exceptions and permission expansion.

## Review Cadence
- Daily/continuous: incidents, failures, critical queue.
- Weekly: mission KPI, escalations, quality, cost, open risk.
- Monthly: role scope, integration health, access review, value realization.
- Quarterly: architecture, governance, autonomy expansion, vendor/platform strategy.

## Expansion Rule
Autonomy expands only after evidence shows acceptable quality, controls, observability and business value at the current level.

# 中文

企业治理的核心不是限制智慧，而是让智慧在明确责任体系内放大。

正式生产环境必须明确：高管 Sponsor、AI 治理负责人、Mission Owner、系统负责人、安全负责人、人工审批人和运营负责人。

每个 Mission 必须有：业务目标、AI 角色、数据范围、工具范围、行动等级、审批规则、升级路径、KPI、部署环境、日志证据和复盘周期。

战略、法律承诺、人事、受监管判断、高风险财务动作、不可逆/破坏性操作、安全例外和权限扩张保留给授权人类。

自主权限只能基于当前层级的质量、治理、可观测和商业价值证据逐级扩展。
