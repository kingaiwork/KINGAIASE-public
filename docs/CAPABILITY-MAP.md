# English

## 1. Core Intelligent-Lifeform Capabilities

KING AI SEA is designed as a persistent intelligent lifeform rather than a single-purpose assistant.

Its public capability model can be summarized as:

**Think · Remember · Plan · Coordinate · Act · Observe · Learn · Evolve · Govern**

### Think
Understand goals, context, priorities, constraints and intent.

### Remember
Preserve useful continuity across conversations, projects, customers, organizations and recurring operations.

### Plan
Turn an objective into steps, milestones, dependencies, decisions and next actions.

### Coordinate
Bring multiple roles, systems, tools and workflows together around one mission.

### Act
Perform approved digital actions rather than only describing what a user should do.

### Observe
Monitor approved signals, task state, business changes, system status and workflow outcomes.

### Learn
Use approved feedback and outcomes to become more aligned with how the owner works.

### Evolve
Improve capabilities and operating patterns through controlled evolution.

### Govern
Operate within owner-defined permissions, approvals, policies and security boundaries.

# 中文

## 1. 智慧生命体核心能力

KING AI SEA 的目标不是做一个单用途 AI 助手，而是构建一个长期存在的智慧生命体。

公开能力可以概括为：

### **思考 · 记忆 · 规划 · 协同 · 行动 · 观察 · 学习 · 进化 · 治理**

### 思考
理解目标、上下文、优先级、约束和真实意图。

### 记忆
围绕对话、项目、客户、企业和持续运营保留真正有价值的长期上下文。

### 规划
把目标转化为步骤、里程碑、依赖关系、决策和下一步行动。

### 协同
围绕一个 Mission 调动不同角色、系统、工具和工作流。

### 行动
在授权范围内真正完成数字工作，而不是只告诉用户“应该怎么做”。

### 观察
关注经过授权的业务变化、任务状态、系统状态和工作结果。

### 学习
通过经过允许的反馈和结果越来越符合主人的工作方式。

### 进化
通过受控进化不断提升长期有用性。

### 治理
始终运行在主人或企业定义的权限、审批、策略和安全边界中。
