# English

## From One-Step Automation to Goal-Driven Work

Traditional automation usually follows a fixed rule: when X happens, do Y.

KING AI SEA can extend this idea into agentic workflows where an approved objective may require understanding context, selecting tools, coordinating specialized roles, waiting for external events, requesting human approval, retrying failed steps, and reporting the final outcome.

## Workflow Types

### One-Time Tasks
Examples:
- analyze a project
- prepare a report
- organize documents
- update approved content
- perform an environment check

### Recurring Work
Examples:
- daily operating brief
- weekly business review
- recurring SEO review
- recurring infrastructure check
- customer follow-up queue
- inventory exception review

### Event-Driven Work
Examples:
- new lead arrives
- payment status changes
- customer submits a support request
- deployment fails
- monitoring alert fires
- new document is added
- inventory falls below a threshold

### Condition-Based Work
Examples:
- notify when a metric crosses a threshold
- escalate when a customer case remains unresolved
- trigger a review when a system changes state
- surface an opportunity when defined business conditions are met

### Long-Running Work
Examples:
- multi-day research
- onboarding workflows
- procurement coordination
- multi-stage content production
- software release coordination
- enterprise approval chains

## Workflow Building Blocks

A public workflow model can include:

- triggers
- context gathering
- planning
- task decomposition
- tool calls
- specialized AI roles
- conditions
- branches
- parallel work
- approvals
- waits
- retries
- checkpoints
- escalation
- reporting
- completion criteria

## Human-in-the-Loop

Not every task should be fully automatic.

Sensitive or high-impact steps can stop for:

- approval
- review
- missing information
- exception handling
- policy checks
- final confirmation

## Workflow Memory

Long-running workflows can benefit from durable state so progress, decisions, approvals and intermediate results are not lost between steps.

Public documentation describes the experience, not the proprietary state architecture.

## Workflow Observability

Operators should be able to understand:

- what is running
- what finished
- what failed
- what is waiting
- what requires approval
- what tool or AI role acted
- what the outcome was

## Workflow Templates

Reusable templates can accelerate deployment for recurring business processes such as customer onboarding, content publishing, sales follow-up, infrastructure checks, reporting, incident response, approvals and service operations.

## The Outcome

**KING AI SEA is designed to reduce the distance between a goal and a completed result — while keeping permissions, approvals and visibility under operator control.**

# 中文

## 从单步自动化升级为目标驱动工作

传统自动化通常是固定规则：发生 X，就执行 Y。

KING AI SEA 可以进一步形成 Agentic Workflow：一个经过授权的目标可能需要理解上下文、选择工具、协调专业 AI 角色、等待外部事件、请求人工批准、对失败步骤重试，并最终汇报结果。

## 工作流类型

### 一次性任务
例如：
- 分析一个项目
- 准备报告
- 整理文档
- 更新经过授权的内容
- 检查运行环境

### 周期性工作
例如：
- 每日运营简报
- 每周经营复盘
- 周期 SEO 检查
- 周期基础设施检查
- 客户跟进队列
- 库存异常复盘

### 事件驱动工作
例如：
- 新线索进入
- 支付状态变化
- 客户提交客服请求
- 部署失败
- 监控告警
- 新文档加入
- 库存低于阈值

### 条件触发工作
例如：
- 指标超过阈值时提醒
- 客户案件长期未解决时升级
- 系统状态变化时触发检查
- 满足业务条件时发现机会

### 长时间任务
例如：
- 多日研究
- onboarding
- 采购协调
- 多阶段内容生产
- 软件发布协调
- 企业多级审批

## 工作流构成

公开能力模型可以包含：

- Trigger
- 上下文收集
- 规划
- 任务拆解
- Tool 调用
- 专业 AI 角色
- 条件
- 分支
- 并行任务
- 审批

- 检查点

- 完成条件

## Human-in-the-Loop

不是所有事情都应该完全自动执行。

敏感或高影响步骤可以因为以下原因暂停：

- 等待批准
- 等待审核
- 缺少信息
- 异常处理
- 策略检查
- 最终确认

## 工作流记忆

长时间工作流需要持久状态，确保进度、决策、审批和中间结果不会因为任务跨时间运行而丢失。

公开资料只描述用户体验，不公开专有状态实现。

## 工作流可观测

运营者应清楚知道：

- 什么正在运行
- 什么已经完成
- 什么失败
- 什么正在等待
- 什么需要审批
- 哪个 Tool / AI 角色执行了动作
- 最终结果是什么

## 工作流模板

可以为客户 onboarding、内容发布、销售跟进、基础设施检查、报表、事件响应、审批和服务运营等重复流程创建可复用模板。

## 最终价值

**KING AI SEA 希望减少“目标”与“完成结果”之间的距离，同时让权限、审批与可见性始终掌握在运营者手中。**
