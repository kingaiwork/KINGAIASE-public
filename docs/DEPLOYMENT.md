# English

## Deployment Designed Around Control

KING AI SEA can be shaped around different infrastructure, privacy, integration, and operating requirements. Public documentation describes deployment choices at a product level and intentionally excludes proprietary implementation details.

## Deployment Paths

### ☁️ Cloud
Suitable for teams that want fast access, centralized management, and integration with cloud services.

### 🖥️ Private VPS
Suitable for founders, independent operators, technical teams, and businesses that want a dedicated environment with clearly defined access and permissions.

### 🏢 Enterprise-Controlled Infrastructure
Suitable for organizations that require tighter governance, internal network boundaries, private knowledge sources, audit requirements, and customized approval flows.

### 🔐 Hybrid
Suitable for organizations that need to combine cloud intelligence with private infrastructure, internal systems, or locally controlled data and services.

## Integration Categories

Depending on authorization and project scope, KING AI SEA can be connected with categories such as:

- websites and web applications
- email and communication systems
- calendars and task systems
- CRM and customer operations
- internal knowledge bases
- APIs and business services
- cloud platforms
- VPS and server environments
- monitoring and reporting systems
- developer tools and source control
- content and publishing workflows
- approved internal business applications

## Permission First

KING AI SEA is designed around a simple principle:

**Automation scope, execution permissions, approval requirements, and security boundaries are defined by the operator.**

A deployment should begin by defining the assets involved, the information the system may access, the actions it may perform, the approvals required, and the recovery path for important operations.

## Architecture Review

A practical deployment normally begins with an architecture review that identifies:

1. business or personal objectives
2. systems and information sources
3. first high-value workflows
4. integration boundaries
5. permissions and approvals
6. monitoring and reporting expectations
7. deployment environment
8. phased expansion path

# 中文

## 围绕“可控”设计部署

KING AI SEA 可以根据不同的基础设施、隐私、集成与运营要求进行部署规划。公开文档只说明产品层面的部署选择，不公开专有核心实现。

## 部署方向

### ☁️ 云端部署
适合希望快速使用、集中管理并连接云服务的团队。

### 🖥️ 私人 VPS
适合创业者、独立经营者、技术团队以及希望拥有独立运行环境并明确控制访问权限的企业。

### 🏢 企业自有基础设施
适合对内部网络边界、私有知识、审计、治理和审批流程有更高要求的组织。

### 🔐 混合架构
适合需要把云端智慧能力与私有基础设施、内部系统、本地数据或本地服务组合起来的组织。

## 可连接的系统类别

在授权和项目范围内，KING AI SEA 可围绕以下类别进行连接：

- 网站和 Web 应用
- 邮件与沟通系统
- 日历和任务系统
- CRM 与客户运营
- 企业内部知识库
- API 与业务服务
- 云平台
- VPS 和服务器环境
- 监控与报表系统
- 开发工具和代码管理
- 内容与发布工作流
- 经授权的企业内部应用

## 权限优先

KING AI SEA 坚持一个核心原则：

**自动化范围、执行权限、审批要求和安全边界由运营者设定。**

一个正式部署应先明确涉及哪些资产、系统能够访问哪些信息、允许执行哪些动作、哪些操作需要批准，以及关键操作发生问题后的恢复路径。

## 架构评估

实际部署通常从架构评估开始，明确：

1. 企业或个人目标
2. 系统和信息源
3. 第一批高价值工作流
4. 集成边界
5. 权限和审批机制
6. 监控与报告需求
7. 部署环境
8. 分阶段扩展路线
