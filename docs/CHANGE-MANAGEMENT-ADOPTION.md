# 🔄 KING AI SEA — Change Management & Adoption

# English

## Adoption Principle
AI Workforce adoption is an operating-model change, not only a software installation.

## Stakeholder Groups
- executives
- mission owners
- frontline users
- IT/security
- data owners
- legal/compliance where relevant
- support/operations

## Adoption Steps
1. define why the mission matters
2. establish baseline
3. explain human vs AI responsibilities
4. start with visible bounded missions
5. train users on escalation and override
6. collect feedback
7. publish KPI/quality evidence
8. expand only after trust is earned

## Communication
Explain what the AI can do, cannot do, what requires approval, how data is used, how to report issues and how humans remain accountable.

# 中文
AI Workforce 的采用不是“装一个软件”，而是运营模式变化。

需要覆盖管理层、Mission Owner、一线用户、IT/安全、数据负责人、必要时的法务/合规以及运营支持。

步骤：说明为什么做 → 建立基线 → 明确人机职责 → 从边界清晰 Mission 开始 → 培训升级/接管 → 收集反馈 → 公开 KPI/质量证据 → 建立信任后扩展。

必须让用户清楚：AI 能做什么、不能做什么、什么要审批、数据怎么使用、如何报告问题以及最终责任仍由谁承担。
