# 🎯 KING AI SEA — Mission Design Standard

# English
A Mission is the governed unit of real work.

## Required Mission Fields
- Name
- Business outcome
- Owner
- Trigger / frequency
- Inputs
- Approved knowledge
- Approved tools
- AI role(s)
- Action level
- Approval checkpoints
- Escalation conditions
- Completion criteria
- KPI
- Cost/latency expectations if relevant
- Evidence / audit requirements
- Failure behavior
- Human takeover path

## Action Levels
1. Observe
2. Research / Analyze
3. Recommend
4. Draft
5. Execute low-risk approved actions
6. Execute with approval
7. Restricted / human-only

## Mission Anti-Patterns
Avoid missions such as “run the company,” “do everything automatically,” or “use all systems.” Break broad goals into measurable bounded missions.

# 中文
Mission 是 KING AI SEA 执行真实工作的受治理基本单位。

每个 Mission 必须定义：名称、业务结果、负责人、触发方式、输入、授权知识、工具、AI 岗位、行动等级、审批点、升级条件、完成标准、KPI、成本/延迟、审计证据、失败行为和人工接管。

行动等级建议：观察 → 研究分析 → 建议 → 草稿 → 低风险授权执行 → 审批后执行 → 人工专属。

禁止使用“自动管理整个公司”“访问所有系统”这类不可治理 Mission，应拆分为可衡量、可授权、可回滚的工作单元。
