# English

## Make Organizational Knowledge Usable

Organizations often have valuable information spread across documents, inboxes, databases, cloud drives, internal applications, CRM systems, policies and individual team members.

KING AI SEA can become an intelligent access layer across approved knowledge sources so users can ask questions, retrieve context, understand history and turn information into action.

## Knowledge Domains

Potential knowledge categories include:

- company policies
- SOPs
- product documentation
- customer knowledge
- project history
- technical documentation
- contracts and administrative records
- sales materials
- marketing assets
- finance documentation
- HR documentation
- support knowledge
- research
- external approved sources

## Knowledge Experiences

### Natural-Language Enterprise Search
Users can ask a question instead of manually searching multiple systems.

### Contextual Answers
Answers can be shaped around role, department, project, customer or workflow context where permissions allow.

### Source-Aware Summaries
Important answers can preserve references to relevant source material so users can verify context.

### Historical Context
Teams can retrieve prior decisions, project changes, recurring issues and operating history.

### Role-Specific Knowledge
Different AI employees can receive only the knowledge appropriate to their responsibilities.

## Data Intelligence

KING AI SEA can also help turn structured business data into understandable operational intelligence.

Potential experiences include:

- KPI summaries
- anomaly explanations
- trend analysis
- customer segmentation support
- operational exceptions
- forecast-supporting analysis
- natural-language reporting
- management dashboards
- cross-system summaries

## Knowledge Freshness

Knowledge quality depends on freshness.

A serious deployment should consider:

- source ownership
- update frequency
- stale-content detection
- archival rules
- version history
- source confidence
- permission changes

## Knowledge Governance

Access should follow organizational boundaries.

Potential controls include:

- source-level permissions
- role-level access
- team boundaries
- sensitive-data classification
- retention policy
- auditability
- source ownership

## Knowledge-to-Action

The most valuable experience is not only answering questions.

For approved workflows, knowledge can become the context for a next action:

**Question → Understand Context → Recommend Next Step → Request Approval → Execute → Record Outcome**

## Public Boundary

The proprietary storage, retrieval, ranking, memory and context-orchestration implementation is intentionally not disclosed.

# 中文

## 让企业知识真正可以被使用

很多企业的重要信息分散在文档、邮箱、数据库、网盘、内部软件、CRM、制度和不同员工脑中。

KING AI SEA 可以成为经过授权的企业知识智慧入口，让员工通过自然语言提问、找回背景、理解历史，并进一步把信息转化成行动。

## 企业知识范围

可以包括：

- 公司制度
- SOP
- 产品资料
- 客户知识
- 项目历史
- 技术文档
- 合同与行政记录
- 销售材料
- 市场素材
- 财务文档
- HR 文档
- 客服知识
- 研究资料
- 经授权的外部信息源

## 知识体验

### 自然语言企业搜索
员工不需要在多个系统里反复搜索，可以直接提问。

### 上下文回答
在权限允许的情况下，可以根据角色、部门、项目、客户和工作流背景提供更相关的答案。

### 带来源意识的摘要
重要回答可以保留相关信息来源，方便用户核对背景。

### 历史上下文
团队可以找到过去决策、项目变化、重复问题和运营历史。

### 岗位知识隔离
不同 AI 员工只访问与自己职责相符的知识。

## 数据智慧

KING AI SEA 也可以帮助把结构化业务数据转化成容易理解的运营智慧。

例如：

- KPI 摘要
- 异常解释
- 趋势分析
- 客户分群辅助
- 运营异常
- 预测辅助分析
- 自然语言报表
- 管理看板
- 跨系统摘要

## 知识新鲜度

知识质量依赖信息是否最新。

正式部署应该考虑：

- 信息源所有人
- 更新频率
- 过期信息识别
- 归档规则
- 版本历史
- 来源可信度
- 权限变化

## 知识治理

访问必须符合企业边界。

可以包括：

- 信息源级权限
- 角色级权限
- 团队边界
- 敏感数据分类
- 保留策略
- 审计
- 信息源所有权

## 从知识走向行动

最高价值不只是回答问题。

在经过授权的工作流中，知识可以成为下一步动作的上下文：

**提问 → 理解背景 → 建议下一步 → 请求审批 → 执行 → 记录结果**

## 公开边界

具体存储、检索、排序、记忆以及上下文调度实现属于专有技术，不在公开仓库披露。
