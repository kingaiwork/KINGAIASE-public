# English

## Intelligence Becomes More Valuable Through Continuity

A useful long-term AI should not behave like a stranger every time it is opened.

KING AI SEA is designed around continuity: the ability to preserve approved context, understand recurring relationships, maintain project awareness, learn working preferences, and become more useful over time.

## Long-Term Context Categories

Depending on deployment policy, useful context may include:

- user preferences
- business terminology
- projects
- recurring tasks
- goals
- approved knowledge
- customer or account context
- past decisions
- workflow history
- relevant outcomes
- communication style
- operating patterns

## Memory as an Experience

The user-facing objective is simple:

- less repeated explanation
- faster context recovery
- better continuity across sessions
- better project awareness
- more personalized support
- stronger long-running workflow coordination

## Organizational Memory

For enterprises, KING AI SEA can help turn fragmented knowledge into an accessible operating memory across approved sources.

This can help new employees understand context faster, help teams find prior decisions, help managers preserve operating knowledge, and reduce the loss of important information across people and systems.

## Evolution OS

Evolution OS is the operating architecture within the unified KING AI SEA system.

Publicly, it represents the idea that the intelligent lifeform should operate continuously across identity, context, tools, workflows, governance and feedback rather than behaving like an isolated model call.

## SAE

SAE is the self-evolution mechanism within KING AI SEA.

At a public product level, SAE represents a controlled improvement loop in which the system can observe approved outcomes, reflect on effectiveness, identify potential improvements, validate changes under defined rules, and improve future usefulness.

The proprietary algorithms, scoring systems, implementation details and internal control logic are not disclosed.

## Controlled Evolution

Evolution should not mean uncontrolled self-modification.

Public governance principles include:

- operator-defined boundaries
- controlled evaluation
- change visibility
- approval for sensitive changes
- rollback where appropriate
- regression checks
- policy constraints
- auditability

## Experience Over Time

A conceptual progression:

### Day 1
Learns the operating context and immediate goals.

### Day 30
Understands recurring tasks, terminology, preferences and common workflows.

### Day 100
Can become deeply integrated into recurring personal or business operating rhythms.

The exact experience depends on deployment, permissions, integrations, usage and approved knowledge.

## The Public Promise

**KING AI SEA is designed to become more useful through continuity — not merely through larger models.**

# 中文

## 智慧的长期价值来自连续性

真正有价值的长期 AI，不应该每次打开都像一个陌生人。

KING AI SEA 强调连续性：在经过授权的前提下保留重要上下文、理解长期关系、掌握项目进度、学习工作偏好，并随着使用时间越来越有价值。

## 长期上下文可以包括

根据部署策略，可以包括：

- 用户偏好
- 企业术语
- 项目
- 重复任务
- 长期目标
- 授权知识
- 客户/账户上下文
- 历史决策
- 工作流历史
- 关键结果
- 沟通方式
- 运营模式

## 记忆带来的体验

用户真正感受到的是：

- 少重复解释
- 更快恢复背景
- 不同会话之间保持连续
- 更了解长期项目
- 更个性化
- 更适合长任务和周期工作

## 企业组织记忆

对于企业，KING AI SEA 可以帮助把分散在不同文档、系统和人员中的授权知识变成更容易访问的组织记忆。

这可以帮助新员工更快理解背景、帮助团队找到过去决策、帮助管理层保留运营知识，并减少重要信息随着人员或系统变化而流失。

## Evolution OS

Evolution OS 是统一 KING AI SEA 系统内部的操作架构。

对外它代表的是：智慧生命体应该围绕身份、上下文、工具、工作流、治理和反馈持续运行，而不是一次一次孤立地调用模型。

## SAE

SAE 是 KING AI SEA 内部的自我进化机制。

在公开产品层，SAE 表示一种受控的持续改进循环：观察经过授权的结果、反思效果、发现可能的优化、在明确规则下验证变化，并提高未来有用性。

具体算法、评分体系、实现方式和内部控制逻辑属于专有技术，不公开。

## 受控进化

进化不等于无控制地自我修改。

公开治理原则包括：

- 用户定义边界
- 受控评估
- 变化可见
- 敏感变化需要审批
- 合适情况下支持回滚
- 回归检查
- 策略约束
- 可审计

## 随时间形成的体验

一个概念性的过程：

### 第一天
理解运行环境和当前目标。

### 第 30 天
开始熟悉重复任务、企业术语、个人偏好和常用工作流。

### 第 100 天
可以逐渐深入到个人或企业的长期运营节奏中。

真实体验取决于部署方式、权限、集成、使用方式和授权知识。

## 对外承诺

**KING AI SEA 的长期价值不仅来自更强模型，也来自持续的理解和连续性。**
