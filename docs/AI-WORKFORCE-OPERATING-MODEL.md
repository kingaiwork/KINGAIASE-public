# English

## 1. One Lifeform, Many Roles

KING AI SEA treats AI Workforce as an extension of one unified intelligent-lifeform system, not as a random collection of disconnected bots.

The public operating model is:

```text
Human Leadership
      ↓
KING AI SEA Intelligent Lifeform
      ↓
Mission & Governance Layer
      ↓
AI Workforce
      ↓
AI Employees / Workflows / Systems
      ↓
Measured Business Outcomes
```

## 2. Five Building Blocks

### Mission
Every AI role should exist for a business or personal outcome, not simply because a model can perform a task.

### Role
Each AI employee has a defined responsibility, owner, scope and expected outputs.

### Authority
Every role receives explicit permission boundaries and escalation rules.

### Workflow
Roles collaborate through repeatable missions, tasks, checkpoints and handoffs.

### Measurement
The organization measures quality, completion, response, cost, customer effect and business results.

## 3. Workforce Structure

A mature AI Workforce can include five functional layers:

### Executive Intelligence
Chief-of-staff support, operating summaries, cross-functional priorities, decision preparation and strategic follow-up.

### Customer & Revenue Intelligence
Customer service, reception, lead qualification, sales follow-up, customer success, marketing and conversion support.

### Operations Intelligence
Task coordination, SOP workflows, procurement, inventory, logistics, scheduling, quality and reporting.

### Technology & Security Intelligence
Developer assistance, QA, DevOps, systems operations, website operations, monitoring, security operations and audit support.

### Corporate Services Intelligence
Finance, invoice workflows, reconciliation, HR, recruiting, onboarding, policy and internal knowledge.

## 4. AI Employee Contract

Every production AI employee should have a public operating contract covering:

- role name
- mission
- accountable human owner
- allowed knowledge
- allowed tools
- allowed actions
- approval requirements
- escalation conditions
- expected outputs
- measurable KPIs
- operating environment
- current lifecycle status

## 5. Workforce Coordination Patterns

### Single Role
One AI employee handles a narrow mission.

### Sequential Team
One role completes work and hands it to the next.

### Specialist Review
One AI produces work, another specialized role checks quality or policy.

### Hub-and-Spoke
A central KING AI coordination experience routes work to specialist roles.

### Human-Gated Workflow
AI employees prepare and execute parts of a process while selected stages require human approval.

### Continuous Operations
AI roles monitor recurring signals and act or escalate based on approved policy.

## 10. Maturity Model

### Level 1 — Assistant
AI supports a human worker.

### Level 2 — Specialized Employee
A defined AI role owns a bounded recurring responsibility.

### Level 3 — Coordinated Team
Multiple AI employees cooperate on shared workflows.

### Level 4 — Governed Workforce
Identity, permissions, approvals, observability and evaluation are centralized.

### Level 5 — Persistent Enterprise Intelligence
AI Workforce becomes one expression of the broader KING AI SEA intelligent-lifeform layer across the organization.

## 11. Operating Principle

### **AI employees are roles. KING AI SEA is the lifeform that gives the workforce continuity, coordination and governance.**

# 中文

## 1. 一个智慧生命体，多种岗位形态

KING AI SEA 不把 AI Workforce 理解成大量彼此割裂的机器人，而是把它视为统一智慧生命体在企业内部延伸出的数字劳动力体系。

公开运营模型：

```text
人类管理层
   ↓
KING AI SEA 智慧生命体
   ↓
Mission 与治理层
   ↓
AI Workforce
   ↓
AI 员工 / 工作流 / 企业系统
   ↓
可衡量业务结果
```

## 2. 五个基础组成

### Mission
每一个 AI 岗位都必须服务于真实业务目标，而不是因为模型“能做”就去做。

### Role
每个 AI 员工必须有明确职责、负责人、范围和预期输出。

### Authority
每个角色必须有明确权限边界和升级规则。

### Workflow
多个 AI 角色通过可重复的 Mission、Task、检查点和交接协同。

### Measurement
企业衡量质量、完成情况、响应、成本、客户影响和业务结果。

## 3. AI Workforce 组织结构

成熟企业可以把 AI Workforce 分成五个功能层：

### 管理智慧
Chief of Staff、经营摘要、跨部门优先级、决策准备和战略跟进。

### 客户与收入智慧
客服、前台、线索筛选、销售跟进、客户成功、市场和转化辅助。

### 运营智慧
任务协调、SOP、采购、库存、物流、排班、质量和报表。

### 技术与安全智慧
开发、QA、DevOps、系统运维、网站运维、监控、安全运营和审计辅助。

### 企业职能智慧
财务、发票、对账、HR、招聘、入职、制度和内部知识。

## 4. 每个 AI 员工的岗位契约

生产环境中的 AI 员工应该至少定义：

- 岗位名称
- Mission
- 人类负责人
- 可使用知识
- 可使用工具
- 可执行动作
- 审批要求
- 升级条件
- 预期输出
- 可衡量 KPI
- 运行环境
- 生命周期状态

## 5. AI Workforce 协同模式

### 单岗位
一个 AI 员工负责一个边界清晰的任务。

### 顺序团队
一个角色完成后交接给下一个角色。

### 专业复核
一个 AI 产出，另一个专业 AI 检查质量或策略。

### Hub-and-Spoke
统一 KING AI 体验负责协调，再把工作分配给专业角色。

### 人工审批工作流
AI 完成准备和部分执行，关键步骤必须由人类批准。

### 持续运营
AI 角色长期观察授权信号，并根据策略执行或升级。

## 10. 成熟度模型

### Level 1 — Assistant
AI 辅助人类员工。

### Level 2 — Specialized Employee
一个明确 AI 岗位负责边界清晰的重复职责。

### Level 3 — Coordinated Team
多个 AI 员工围绕共享工作流协同。

### Level 4 — Governed Workforce
身份、权限、审批、可观测和评估被统一管理。

### Level 5 — Persistent Enterprise Intelligence
AI Workforce 成为 KING AI SEA 在企业内部长期智慧层的一种外在形态。

## 11. 核心运营原则

### **AI 员工是岗位，KING AI SEA 才是赋予整支数字劳动力连续性、协调能力和治理能力的智慧生命体。**
