# English

## Title

**How [Organization / Anonymous Industry Client] Used KING AI SEA to Improve [Mission Outcome]**

## 1. Context

- industry
- organization type
- operating environment
- relevant team / process

Do not disclose confidential customer information.

## 2. Challenge

Describe the actual business problem:

- fragmented workflow
- response delays
- manual coordination
- information retrieval burden
- repetitive tasks
- limited after-hours coverage
- operational visibility gaps

## 3. Mission

Define the exact mission assigned to KING AI SEA.

## 4. Public Solution Design

Describe only high-level components:

- intelligent-lifeform experience
- AI Workforce roles
- approved knowledge
- integrations
- workflow / approval
- human ownership
- observability

## 5. Deployment

State factual deployment pattern only if approved: cloud, VPS, private, hybrid or customer-controlled.

## 6. Results

Use measured evidence only:

- before / after period
- measurement method
- sample size where relevant
- business KPI
- limitations

Never fabricate percentages.

## 7. Human + AI Operating Model

Explain what humans retained ownership of and where AI assisted or executed within permission.

## 8. Trust & Governance

Summarize permission, approval, escalation and monitoring at a public level.

## 9. Lessons

What worked, what changed, what remained human-led and what could be expanded next.

## 10. Status

Label capabilities as Available / Custom-by-Scope / In Development / Planned where relevant.

# 中文

案例必须使用真实且经过客户允许的信息。

标准结构：背景 → 挑战 → Mission → 公开解决方案 → 部署 → 可验证结果 → 人机协作 → 信任治理 → 经验 → 下一步。

结果必须注明真实时间范围、测量方法和限制，禁止虚构“节省 80% 成本”“效率提高 300%”等数字。

公开架构只讲智慧生命体、AI Workforce、授权知识、集成、审批、人类负责人和可观测，不泄露核心技术。
