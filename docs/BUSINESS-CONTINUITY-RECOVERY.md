# ♻️ KING AI SEA — Business Continuity & Recovery

# English

## Continuity Questions
- What happens if AI service is unavailable?
- Can humans continue the process manually?
- What happens when one integration fails?
- What state must be preserved for long-running missions?
- What data/configuration requires backup?
- Who decides restore/re-enable?

## Recovery Priorities
1. safety and authorization
2. preserve evidence/state
3. restore critical customer/business process
4. reconcile incomplete actions
5. validate before resume
6. communicate status
7. add regression/evaluation coverage

# 中文
连续性必须回答：AI 不可用时人工能否继续、单个集成故障怎么办、长任务需要保留什么状态、哪些数据/配置要备份、谁决定恢复和重新启用。

恢复优先级：安全与授权 → 保存证据/状态 → 恢复关键业务 → 对账未完成动作 → 验证 → 沟通 → 增加回归/评估覆盖。
