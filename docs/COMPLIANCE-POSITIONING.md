# ⚖️ KING AI SEA — Compliance Positioning Guide

> Public communication guide, not legal advice and not a certification claim.

# English

## Principles
- distinguish product controls from legal compliance
- do not claim certification unless independently verified and current
- map customer requirements to deployment/control evidence
- keep regulated decisions with appropriately authorized humans/systems
- document data scope, retention, access and audit where required

## High-Level Topics Customers May Ask About
- privacy
- data residency
- access control
- auditability
- retention/deletion
- incident response
- vendor/subprocessor management
- sector-specific rules
- records management
- human oversight

## Safe Public Language
Use: “designed to support configurable permissions, approvals, audit and deployment boundaries.”

Avoid: “automatically compliant with every law,” “fully certified” or “guaranteed compliance” without evidence.

# 中文
本文件是公开合规表述指南，不是法律意见，也不代表任何认证。

必须区分“产品具有某种控制能力”和“企业已经满足某项法律/认证”。认证只有在独立验证且仍有效时才能声称。

常见主题包括隐私、数据驻留、访问控制、审计、保留/删除、事件响应、供应商、行业规则、记录管理和人工监督。

建议表述：“支持可配置权限、审批、审计和部署边界。” 禁止无证据声称“自动符合所有法律”“完全认证”“保证合规”。
