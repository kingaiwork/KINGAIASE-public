# English

## 1. Trust Is a Product Capability

KING AI SEA is designed around a simple principle:

### **More intelligence should come with more control, not less.**

A persistent intelligent lifeform may participate in communication, operations, knowledge work, automation, customer service, software, infrastructure and business workflows. That makes trust, permission and visibility first-class product capabilities.

## 2. Public Trust Model

The public trust model includes:

- **Identity** — who or what is acting.
- **Role** — what responsibility the intelligence is assigned.
- **Scope** — what systems, data or workflows it may access.
- **Permission** — what it may read, draft, recommend, execute or approve.
- **Approval** — when a human decision is required.
- **Auditability** — what happened, when and under whose authority.
- **Observability** — current task, status, failure, latency, cost and outcome visibility.
- **Escalation** — when the system should stop and request human intervention.
- **Environment boundaries** — separation between personal, development, staging, production and enterprise environments.
- **Data boundaries** — user- and organization-defined handling of approved information.

## 3. Human Authority

KING AI SEA is designed so the owner or organization defines the automation range, execution permissions and security boundaries.

A public permission vocabulary can include:

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Read | View approved information |
| Analyze | Interpret and summarize |
| Recommend | Propose a next action |
| Draft | Prepare content or changes without applying them |
| Execute | Perform an approved action |
| Approve | Approve lower-risk delegated actions where policy allows |
| Escalate | Stop and request human review |
| Deny | Explicitly block an action |

Sensitive or high-impact operations can require additional approval, policy or environmental restrictions.

## 4. Governance Across an AI Workforce

For enterprises, trust must scale beyond a single assistant. A governed AI Workforce needs visibility into:

- AI employee identity
- assigned role and owner
- permitted systems
- permitted actions
- active missions
- pending approvals
- execution history
- quality signals
- cost and resource use
- incidents and exceptions
- deployment environment
- policy status

This is why the public KING AI SEA product vision includes a unified **AI Control Center**.

## 5. Security Concepts

Within the unified KING AI SEA system:

- **ACRE** represents the active defense and immune-system security concept.
- **Root Policy Kernel** represents high-authority policy and execution boundaries.
- **Governance & Trust** represents the public product layer for permissions, approvals, policy, audit and human authority.

The proprietary implementation of these systems is not disclosed in this repository.

## 6. Data & Deployment Choices

The public deployment model may include:

- cloud deployment
- dedicated VPS deployment
- private server deployment
- enterprise private environment
- hybrid architecture
- local intelligence integration
- isolated environments for sensitive workloads

The appropriate architecture depends on operational, privacy, security, regulatory and integration requirements.

## 7. Trust Questions Enterprises Should Ask

Before deploying an AI workforce, an organization should be able to answer:

1. Who owns each AI role?
2. What data can it access?
3. What tools can it use?
4. What actions can it execute?
5. Which actions require approval?
6. How are actions logged?
7. How are failures surfaced?
8. How can a human take over?
9. How are environments separated?
10. How is access removed?
11. How are quality and business outcomes measured?
12. Which capabilities are available now versus planned?

## 8. Public Disclosure Boundary

This Trust Center intentionally does **not** disclose:

- internal detection rules
- security signatures
- privileged execution logic
- secret-management implementation
- internal policy-engine code
- authentication secrets
- production network topology
- internal database structure
- proprietary prompts
- agent-routing internals
- memory implementation
- SAE evolution algorithms

The goal is transparency about **control and responsibility** without exposing security-sensitive implementation.

# 中文

## 1. 信任本身就是产品能力

KING AI SEA 的核心原则之一是：

### **智慧越强，控制必须越清晰。**

一个长期存在的智慧生命体，未来可能参与沟通、运营、知识工作、自动化、客户服务、软件、基础设施以及企业业务流程。因此，权限、治理、审计和可见性不能作为附加功能，而必须成为智慧生命体系统的一部分。

## 2. 公开信任模型

KING AI SEA 对外公开的信任体系包括：

- **身份 Identity** — 谁在执行。
- **角色 Role** — 这个智慧角色负责什么。
- **范围 Scope** — 可以接触哪些系统、数据和工作流。
- **权限 Permission** — 可以读取、分析、建议、草拟还是执行。
- **审批 Approval** — 哪些动作必须由人类确认。
- **审计 Auditability** — 什么时间、以什么权限、执行了什么。
- **可观测 Observability** — 任务、状态、失败、成本、质量和结果是否可见。
- **升级 Escalation** — 什么时候停止自动执行并交给人类。
- **环境边界** — 个人、开发、测试、生产、企业环境如何区分。
- **数据边界** — 用户与企业如何定义授权数据范围。

## 3. 人类最终权力

KING AI SEA 的自动化范围、执行权限与安全边界由使用者或组织设定。

公开权限语义可理解为：

| 层级 | 含义 |
|---|---|
| Read | 读取经过授权的信息 |
| Analyze | 分析与总结 |
| Recommend | 提出下一步建议 |
| Draft | 草拟内容或变更，但不直接应用 |
| Execute | 执行经过授权的动作 |
| Approve | 在策略允许范围内批准低风险委托动作 |
| Escalate | 停止并请求人工处理 |
| Deny | 明确禁止执行 |

敏感或高影响操作可以要求更严格的审批、策略和环境限制。

## 4. AI Workforce 的企业治理

企业不能只管理一个 AI，而需要管理一整支数字劳动力。

因此企业需要看到：

- 每个 AI 员工是谁
- 谁负责它
- 它的岗位是什么
- 它能访问哪些系统
- 它能执行哪些动作
- 当前正在做什么
- 哪些任务等待审批
- 历史执行记录
- 质量与业务结果
- 成本和资源消耗
- 异常和事件
- 部署在哪个环境
- 当前策略状态

这也是 KING AI SEA 为什么需要统一 **AI Control Center** 的原因。

## 5. 安全概念

在统一 KING AI SEA 体系内部：

- **ACRE** 代表主动防御与免疫系统安全概念。
- **Root Policy Kernel** 代表高权限策略和执行边界概念。
- **Governance & Trust** 是对外呈现权限、审批、策略、审计和人类最终权力的产品层。

这些系统的专有内部实现不会在公开仓库中披露。

## 6. 数据与部署选择

公开部署形态可以包括：

- 云部署
- 独立 VPS
- 私有服务器
- 企业私有环境
- 混合部署
- 本地模型/本地智慧接入
- 敏感业务隔离环境

实际选择取决于业务、隐私、安全、监管和系统集成需求。

## 7. 企业采购前应该问的问题

部署 AI Workforce 前，企业至少应该能够回答：

1. 每一个 AI 角色由谁负责？
2. 它能访问什么数据？
3. 它能调用什么工具？
4. 它能执行什么动作？
5. 哪些动作必须审批？
6. 执行是否有记录？
7. 失败如何被发现？
8. 人类如何接管？
9. 不同环境如何隔离？
10. 权限如何撤销？
11. 如何衡量质量和业务结果？
12. 哪些能力已经可用，哪些仍处于开发或规划？

## 8. 公开披露边界

Trust Center 不会公开：

- 内部检测规则
- 安全特征
- 高权限执行逻辑
- 密钥管理实现
- 策略引擎代码
- 身份认证秘密
- 生产网络拓扑
- 内部数据库结构
- 私有 Prompt
- Agent Routing 内部逻辑
- Memory 内部实现
- SAE 自进化算法

我们的目标是公开 **控制原则、责任边界与产品能力**，而不是暴露可以被复制或攻击的核心实现。
