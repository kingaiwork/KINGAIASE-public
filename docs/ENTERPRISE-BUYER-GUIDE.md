# English

## 1. What Are You Actually Buying?

An enterprise should not evaluate KING AI SEA as a single chatbot or isolated AI feature.

The strategic question is whether the organization wants to build a persistent intelligence layer that can coordinate people, AI employees, knowledge, workflows, applications and approved digital systems around real business objectives.

KING AI SEA can be evaluated across six dimensions:

1. **Intelligence** — understands goals, context and business priorities.
2. **Continuity** — preserves useful context over time.
3. **Action** — participates in approved real-world digital work.
4. **Workforce** — coordinates specialized AI roles.
5. **Governance** — provides permissions, approvals, audit and human authority.
6. **Deployment** — supports a deployment model appropriate to the organization.

## 2. Common Enterprise Entry Points

Organizations do not need to transform everything at once. Typical entry points include:

- AI customer service
- AI sales development and follow-up
- executive briefing and decision support
- internal knowledge intelligence
- AI website receptionist
- AI operations coordination
- IT / DevOps assistance
- document and reporting workflows
- invoice and finance operations
- HR onboarding and policy assistance
- long-running recurring workflows
- private AI environment

## 3. Buy for Outcomes, Not Demos

A good evaluation should define measurable operational outcomes before selecting features.

Examples:

- reduce first-response time
- increase qualified leads
- reduce manual follow-up
- improve operational visibility
- reduce repetitive admin work
- shorten report preparation time
- improve knowledge access
- reduce deployment or incident-response friction
- improve customer handoff quality
- reduce missed recurring tasks

## 5. Suggested Adoption Journey

### Phase 1 — Focused Mission
Choose one high-value, low-ambiguity workflow with clear ownership and measurable outcomes.

### Phase 2 — Connected Intelligence
Connect approved knowledge, communication channels, business tools and structured workflows.

### Phase 3 — AI Workforce
Introduce multiple specialized AI employees around customer, revenue, operations or technology workflows.

### Phase 4 — Control Plane
Centralize agent identity, mission status, approvals, governance, quality and cost visibility.

### Phase 5 — Persistent Enterprise Intelligence
Expand into a continuous intelligence layer across departments and recurring operations.

## 6. Deployment Decision Guide

### Cloud
Best when speed, simplicity and broad accessibility are priorities.

### Dedicated VPS
Useful when a dedicated runtime and stronger operational isolation are desirable.

### Private Server / Private Cloud
Useful for organizations with stricter control, data-boundary or infrastructure requirements.

### Hybrid
Useful when some intelligence is cloud-based while selected data, systems or workloads remain private/local.

## 7. Build vs Buy vs Integrate

Enterprises should consider three approaches:

### Build Everything Internally
Maximum customization, but highest engineering, governance and maintenance burden.

### Buy Isolated AI Tools
Fast for narrow tasks, but can create fragmented identity, duplicated knowledge and disconnected workflows.

### Build on a Unified Intelligent-Lifeform Platform
Aims to preserve one governance model, one intelligence relationship and a coordinated AI Workforce while still allowing integrations and custom industry experiences.

## 8. Questions for Architecture Review

A KING AI SEA architecture discussion should define:

- business mission
- users and owners
- existing systems
- required AI roles
- approved tools
- knowledge sources
- sensitive actions
- approval points
- deployment preference
- availability requirements
- reporting and observability needs
- success metrics

# 中文

## 1. 企业真正采购的是什么？

企业不应该把 KING AI SEA 当成一个聊天机器人或单一 AI 功能来评估。

真正需要判断的是：企业是否希望建立一个能够长期存在的智慧层，把人类员工、AI 员工、知识、业务流程、软件应用和经过授权的数字系统围绕真实业务目标连接起来。

企业可以从六个维度评估 KING AI SEA：

1. **智慧** — 是否能理解目标、上下文和业务优先级。
2. **连续性** — 是否能长期保留有价值上下文。
3. **行动** — 是否能参与经过授权的真实数字工作。
4. **AI Workforce** — 是否能协调多个专业 AI 角色。
5. **治理** — 是否具备权限、审批、审计和人类最终权力。
6. **部署** — 是否能适应企业自己的基础设施和隐私需求。

## 2. 常见企业切入点

企业不需要一次改造所有业务。常见起点包括：

- AI 客服
- AI 销售开发与跟进
- 管理层日报和决策辅助
- 企业内部知识智慧
- AI 官网前台
- AI 运营协调
- IT / DevOps 辅助
- 文档和报表工作流
- 发票和财务运营
- HR 入职与制度助手
- 长期周期性任务
- 私有 AI 环境

## 3. 采购结果，而不是 Demo

真正有价值的企业评估必须先定义业务结果。

例如：

- 降低首次响应时间
- 提高有效销售线索
- 减少人工跟进
- 提高运营可见性
- 减少重复行政工作
- 缩短报表准备时间

- 降低部署和故障处理摩擦

## 5. 推荐采用路径

### 第一阶段 — 聚焦一个 Mission
选择一个价值高、边界清楚、负责人明确、可以衡量结果的流程。

### 第二阶段 — 连接企业智慧
接入经过授权的知识、沟通渠道、业务工具和结构化工作流。

### 第三阶段 — AI Workforce
围绕客户、销售、运营或技术工作引入多个专业 AI 员工。

### 第四阶段 — Control Plane
统一管理身份、任务、审批、治理、质量和成本。

### 第五阶段 — 企业持续智慧
从单点流程扩展为跨部门、跨系统、长期运行的企业智慧层。

## 6. 部署选择

### Cloud
适合优先考虑上线速度、简单性和广泛访问的场景。

### Dedicated VPS
适合希望拥有独立运行环境和更强运营隔离的企业。

### Private Server / Private Cloud
适合对数据边界、基础设施控制或内部政策要求更高的组织。

### Hybrid
适合部分智慧在云端运行，而部分数据、系统或工作负载保留在私有/本地环境的企业。

## 7. 自研、购买单点工具还是统一平台？

### 全部自研
定制能力最强，但工程、治理和长期维护成本也最高。

### 购买大量单点 AI 工具
短期上线快，但容易形成多个身份、重复知识、割裂工作流和分散治理。

### 基于统一智慧生命体平台建设
目标是在保持统一治理、统一智慧关系和 AI Workforce 协同的同时，仍然允许企业集成自己的系统并开发行业专属体验。

## 8. 架构评估应该确定什么？

- 业务目标
- 使用者和负责人
- 现有系统
- 所需 AI 角色
- 允许调用的工具
- 知识来源
- 敏感动作
- 审批节点
- 部署偏好
- 可用性要求
- 报表和可观测需求
- 成功指标
