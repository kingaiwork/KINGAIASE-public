# 🤝 KING AI SEA — Partner Certification Framework

> Ecosystem blueprint. Certification names are only used publicly after a real program, criteria and verification process exist.

# English

## Partner Tracks

1. Technology Partner
2. Integration Partner
3. Solution Partner
4. Industry Partner
5. Deployment Partner
6. Security & Governance Partner
7. Developer Partner
8. Training / Enablement Partner
9. Strategic Partner

## Proposed Levels

### Registered
Basic ecosystem participation and brand-policy acceptance.

### Qualified
Demonstrated product knowledge, documented delivery capability and completed evaluation.

### Certified
Validated implementation capability, governance knowledge, support process and reference architecture competency.

### Strategic
Joint roadmap, major integration, regional/industry investment or significant go-to-market commitment.

## Certification Domains

- KING AI SEA positioning and unified-system model
- intelligent-lifeform public architecture
- AI Workforce design
- AI Employee role design
- integrations
- deployment
- Trust Center principles
- approval / human override
- evaluation and KPI
- industry solution design
- SEO/GEO entity consistency for public deployments

## Required Evidence

Potential evidence may include:

- training completion
- architecture exercise
- solution design review
- security / governance assessment
- pilot implementation
- support readiness
- customer-approved case study where permitted

## Brand Rules

Partners must not:

- claim access to undisclosed core technology
- describe Evolution OS / SAE / ACRE as separate competing products
- invent certifications or performance claims
- publish sensitive implementation details
- imply permission to use customer data beyond approved scope

# 中文

合作伙伴体系建议分为：技术、集成、解决方案、行业、部署、安全治理、开发者、培训和战略伙伴。

未来等级可设计为：**Registered / Qualified / Certified / Strategic**，但只有真正建立审核制度后才能公开使用“认证”称号。

认证内容覆盖：KING AI SEA 统一系统定位、智慧生命体公开架构、AI Workforce、AI 员工、集成、部署、Trust、审批与人类接管、评估、KPI、行业方案和 SEO/GEO 实体一致性。

合作伙伴禁止虚构访问核心技术、拆分品牌体系、制造虚假认证/性能数据、公开安全敏感实现或超范围使用客户数据。
