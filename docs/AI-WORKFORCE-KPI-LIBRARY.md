# English

## 1. Why KPI Design Matters

AI Workforce success should not be measured by message count, token volume or how “human-like” an agent sounds. The useful question is whether the intelligent lifeform creates measurable business outcomes while remaining governed.

## 2. KPI Families

### A. Mission Outcomes
- Mission completion rate
- On-time completion rate
- Successful outcome rate
- Exception rate
- Rework rate
- Escalation rate
- Human takeover rate
- Average mission duration
- Time-to-first-action
- Time-to-resolution

### B. Human Leverage
- Human hours saved
- Administrative work reduced
- Repetitive steps eliminated
- Manager review time reduced
- Analyst preparation time reduced
- Employee capacity released for higher-value work
- Percentage of workflow automated within approved scope

### C. Customer Experience
- First-response time
- Resolution time
- Self-service completion rate
- Escalation accuracy
- Customer satisfaction where measured legitimately
- Abandonment reduction
- Booking or inquiry completion rate
- Follow-up completion
- Knowledge-answer accuracy

### D. Revenue & Growth
- Qualified leads created
- Lead response time
- Follow-up coverage
- Opportunity progression
- Proposal/quote turnaround time
- Conversion assistance rate
- Upsell/cross-sell opportunities surfaced
- Revenue influenced by AI-assisted workflows

### E. Operations
- Workflow cycle time
- SLA compliance
- Exception detection time
- Backlog reduction
- Task routing accuracy
- Inventory/dispatch exception resolution
- Maintenance coordination time
- Process completion consistency

### F. Finance
- Invoice cycle time
- Reconciliation time
- Exception identification rate
- Accounts-receivable follow-up coverage
- Reporting preparation time
- Manual data-entry reduction
- Cost per processed transaction/work item

### G. Technology & IT
- Incident triage time
- Deployment preparation time
- Mean time to acknowledge
- Mean time to recovery where applicable
- Repetitive operational tasks reduced
- Documentation coverage
- Change preparation accuracy
- Human approval turnaround

### H. Security & Governance
- Unauthorized-action attempts blocked
- Sensitive-action approval rate
- Policy violations detected
- Audit completeness
- Escalation response time
- Human override success
- Identity/permission exceptions
- Security event closure time

### I. Quality
- Task acceptance rate
- Human correction rate
- Factual accuracy on evaluated tasks
- Policy adherence
- Output completeness
- Consistency across repeated tasks
- Hallucination/error rate on controlled evaluations
- Regression rate after changes

### J. Efficiency & Cost
- Cost per mission
- Cost per successful outcome
- Cost per customer interaction
- Cost per qualified lead
- Cost per workflow
- Compute/model cost trend
- Human + AI blended cost
- Savings versus baseline process

### K. Continuity & Intelligence
- Context reuse rate
- Repeated-information requests reduced
- Mission continuity across sessions
- Knowledge freshness coverage
- Long-term project recall accuracy on evaluated cases
- Cross-role coordination success

### L. Evolution & Improvement
- Validated improvement proposals
- Accepted improvements
- Regression-free improvements
- Quality uplift after approved change
- Reduced error rate over time
- Reduced escalation rate where appropriate

## 3. Executive Scorecard

A simple executive view should focus on:

**Value** — revenue, cost, time, service quality.  
**Control** — approvals, incidents, policy adherence.  
**Quality** — acceptance, error, rework.  
**Scale** — missions, roles, departments, integrations.  
**Efficiency** — cost per outcome and human leverage.  
**Trust** — auditability and exception visibility.

## 4. KPI Design Rules

1. Measure outcomes, not activity vanity metrics.
2. Establish a human/process baseline before rollout.
3. Separate AI-assisted and AI-operated workflows.
4. Track exceptions and failures, not only successes.
5. Segment metrics by mission and risk level.
6. Keep high-risk decisions human-accountable.
7. Do not optimize cost at the expense of correctness or safety.
8. Review KPIs as workflows evolve.

# 中文

## 1. 为什么必须建立 KPI？

AI Workforce 不能用“发了多少消息、用了多少 Token、说话多像人”来判断成功。

真正应该衡量的是：**它是否在明确治理边界内创造可衡量的业务结果。**

## 2. KPI 十二大类

### A. Mission 结果
任务完成率、按时完成率、成功结果率、异常率、返工率、升级率、人工接管率、平均任务时长、首次行动时间、解决时间。

### B. 人类杠杆效应
节省工时、减少行政工作、消除重复步骤、减少管理审核时间、减少分析准备时间、释放员工高价值工作能力、在授权范围内的自动化比例。

### C. 客户体验
首次响应、解决时间、自助完成率、升级准确度、真实测量的客户满意度、流失/放弃率、预约/询盘完成、跟进完成、知识答案准确度。

### D. 收入与增长
有效线索、响应速度、跟进覆盖、机会推进、报价速度、转化辅助、增购机会、AI 辅助工作流影响的收入。

### E. 运营
流程周期、SLA、异常发现时间、积压减少、任务路由准确度、库存/调度异常处理、维修协调时间、流程一致性。

### F. 财务
发票周期、对账时间、异常识别、应收跟进覆盖、报表准备时间、人工录入减少、每笔业务处理成本。

### G. 技术与 IT
事件分流时间、部署准备时间、响应/恢复指标、重复运维减少、文档覆盖、变更准备准确度、审批速度。

### H. 安全与治理
阻止未授权动作、敏感操作审批、策略违规、审计完整度、升级响应、人工覆盖成功率、权限异常、安全事件关闭时间。

### I. 质量
任务接受率、人工修改率、受控评估准确率、策略遵循、完整性、一致性、受控幻觉/错误率、升级后的回归率。

### J. 效率与成本
每个 Mission 成本、每个成功结果成本、每个客户交互成本、每条有效线索成本、每个流程成本、模型/算力成本趋势、人机混合成本、相对基线节省。

### K. 连续性与智慧
上下文复用、重复提问减少、跨会话任务连续、知识新鲜度、长期项目记忆准确度、跨岗位协同成功率。

### L. 进化与提升
经过验证的改进建议、被接受的改进、无回归改进、质量提升、错误率下降、适当场景下的升级率下降。

## 3. 管理层看板

建议只看六类：

**价值、控制、质量、规模、效率、信任。**

## 4. KPI 原则

建立真实基线，不用虚荣指标；区分 AI 辅助和 AI 执行；统计失败与异常；按照 Mission 和风险等级拆分；高风险决策保持人类负责；不能为了降低成本牺牲正确性与安全。

## 5. 不虚构数据

所有 KPI 目标必须来自真实客户或真实 Pilot 基线。本公开文件不提供虚构的准确率、节省比例、收入增长或客户满意度数字。
