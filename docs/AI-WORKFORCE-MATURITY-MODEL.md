# English

## Level 0 — Traditional Digital Operations

Characteristics:
- Human-driven software
- Manual coordination
- Siloed data and tools
- Automation limited to deterministic rules

Goal: identify repetitive, measurable missions.

## Level 1 — AI Assistance

Characteristics:
- Chat, search, drafting and summarization
- Human initiates and completes most work
- Limited integration
- Low execution authority

Goal: improve quality and human leverage without increasing risk.

## Level 2 — Connected Intelligence

Characteristics:
- Approved enterprise knowledge
- CRM/email/calendar/app connections
- Role-aware assistance
- Mission status and context continuity

Goal: move from isolated answers to connected business context.

## Level 3 — Governed Agentic Workflows

Characteristics:
- Multi-step workflows
- Human approval checkpoints
- Event/scheduled missions
- Exception handling
- Audit and observability

Goal: move from assistance to governed coordination and action.

## Level 4 — AI Workforce

Characteristics:
- Multiple specialized AI employees
- Shared missions
- Cross-role handoffs
- Defined human owners
- KPI-based evaluation
- AI Control Center visibility

Goal: operate a coherent digital workforce rather than isolated agents.

## Level 5 — Enterprise Intelligent Lifeform

Characteristics:
- Persistent organizational continuity
- Unified identity and governance model
- Cross-department mission coordination
- Broad but bounded digital action
- Enterprise knowledge and operating context
- Continuous measurement and controlled adaptation

Goal: establish KING AI SEA as a persistent intelligence layer across the organization.

## Level 6 — Controlled Evolution at Scale

Characteristics:
- Validated improvement loops
- Regression-aware change management
- Policy-governed evolution
- Cross-environment learning where permitted
- Mature trust, evaluation and observability

Goal: increase system usefulness over time while preserving owner-defined authority.

## Maturity Dimensions

Organizations should score themselves separately across:

1. Business Mission Clarity
2. Knowledge Readiness
3. Integration Readiness
4. Identity & Role Design
5. Permission & Approval Governance
6. Workflow Capability
7. AI Workforce Coordination
8. Observability
9. Evaluation & Quality
10. Security & Trust
11. Cost Management
12. Change Management
13. Executive Governance
14. Controlled Improvement

## Important Principle

Maturity is not the same as maximum autonomy. A highly mature organization may intentionally keep sensitive missions human-led while automating lower-risk work deeply.

# 中文

## Level 0 — 传统数字运营

软件由人操作、流程靠人工协调、数据与工具彼此割裂，自动化主要是固定规则。

## Level 1 — AI 辅助

AI 用于聊天、搜索、起草和总结，人类仍然发起并完成大部分工作，系统连接和执行权限较少。

## Level 2 — Connected Intelligence

开始连接授权企业知识、CRM、邮件、日历和应用，AI 拥有角色上下文和 Mission 连续性。

## Level 3 — 受治理 Agentic Workflow

出现多步骤工作流、人工审批、事件/定时 Mission、异常处理、审计和可观测。

## Level 4 — AI Workforce

多个专业 AI 员工围绕共享 Mission 协作，有人类负责人、跨岗位交接、KPI 评估和 Control Center 可见性。

## Level 5 — 企业智慧生命体

形成企业长期连续性、统一身份与治理、跨部门 Mission 协同、广泛但有边界的数字行动、企业知识和运营上下文。

## Level 6 — 规模化受控进化

具备经过验证的改进循环、回归控制、策略治理进化、成熟的信任、评估和可观测体系。

## 十四个成熟度维度

业务 Mission、知识、集成、身份岗位、权限审批、工作流、AI Workforce 协同、可观测、评估质量、安全信任、成本、变更管理、高管治理、受控提升。

## 关键原则

**成熟度不等于最大自治。** 真正成熟的企业可以故意让高风险 Mission 保持人类主导，同时对低风险重复工作进行更深度自动化。
