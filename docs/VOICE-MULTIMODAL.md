# English

## Intelligence Should Feel Natural

Human communication is not limited to typing. KING AI SEA can extend intelligent-lifeform experiences across voice, images, documents, screens and other approved multimodal inputs.

## Voice Intelligence

Potential experiences include:

- real-time conversational voice
- AI phone receptionist
- customer service by voice
- appointment and reservation calls
- voice-based personal assistant
- voice task capture
- voice summaries and briefings
- multilingual voice interaction
- escalation from AI voice to a human team

## Visual Intelligence

Potential experiences include:

- image understanding
- screenshot analysis
- document/photo intake
- visual issue identification
- product/image assistance
- visual workflow initiation
- structured information extraction

## Document Intelligence

KING AI SEA can help users work with approved documents such as:

- contracts and agreements for administrative understanding
- invoices and receipts
- forms
- reports
- policies
- manuals
- presentations
- spreadsheets
- customer records
- project documents

Professional or regulated decisions should remain subject to appropriate qualified review.

## Screen-Aware Assistance

In approved environments, intelligence can assist users while they work inside applications by understanding the current screen or digital context and helping identify the next action.

## Multimodal Workflow

A workflow may combine multiple modes:

**Voice request → Document intake → Visual understanding → Business system action → Human approval → Report**

## Accessibility

Multimodal interfaces can also improve accessibility by giving users alternative ways to interact with digital services through voice, text and visual understanding.

## The Experience Goal

**KING AI SEA should meet people in the communication mode that is most natural for the moment — not force every task into a chat box.**

# 中文

## 智慧应该以最自然的方式与人交流

人类交流从来不只是打字。KING AI SEA 可以把智慧生命体体验延伸到语音、图片、文档、屏幕以及其他经过授权的多模态输入中。

## 语音智慧

可以包括：

- 实时语音对话
- AI 电话前台
- 语音客服
- 电话预约与预订
- 个人语音助理
- 语音记录任务
- 语音简报
- 多语言语音
- AI 电话转人工

## 视觉智慧

可以包括：

- 图片理解
- 截图分析
- 文档/照片录入
- 视觉问题识别
- 商品图片辅助
- 通过图片触发业务流程
- 结构化信息提取

## 文档智慧

在经过授权的情况下，可以辅助处理：

- 合同/协议的行政理解
- 发票与收据
- 表单
- 报告
- 制度
- 手册
- PPT
- 表格
- 客户记录
- 项目文档

涉及专业或受监管的判断时，应由相应合格专业人士审核。

## 屏幕理解辅助

在经过授权的环境中，KING AI SEA 可以理解用户当前正在使用的软件界面和数字上下文，并帮助判断下一步操作。

## 多模态工作流

一个工作流可以同时结合多种输入输出：

**语音提出目标 → 上传文档 → 视觉/文档理解 → 调用业务系统 → 人工审批 → 输出报告**

## 无障碍体验

多模态交互也可以通过语音、文字和视觉理解，为不同用户提供更灵活的数字服务方式。

## 最终体验目标

**KING AI SEA 应该在最适合当前场景的沟通方式中与人协作，而不是把所有事情都塞进一个聊天框。**
