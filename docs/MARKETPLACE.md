# English

## Extend the Intelligent Lifeform

A mature KING AI SEA ecosystem can allow customers and developers to extend the intelligent lifeform with approved agents, AI employees, skills, workflows, integrations, knowledge packs and industry solutions.

The marketplace is not a place for unreviewed automation to gain uncontrolled access. Every extension should declare what it does, what it needs, and what it can access.

## Marketplace Categories

### AI Employees
- customer service
- sales
- marketing
- operations
- IT
- security
- finance
- HR
- executive support

### Industry Agents
- hospitality
- logistics
- property management
- healthcare administration
- senior-care administration
- real estate
- retail
- restaurants
- education
- SaaS
- manufacturing
- field service
- travel

### Skills
Reusable capabilities for specific actions or workflows.

### Workflow Packs
Predefined process templates for recurring business outcomes.

### Integrations
Approved connectors to external tools, services and business systems.

### Knowledge Packs
Structured public or organization-provided knowledge packages for a domain or workflow.

### Website & App Components
Embeddable intelligent experiences for websites, mobile apps and SaaS products.

## Extension Manifest

A high-quality marketplace item should declare:

- name
- publisher
- version
- purpose
- required permissions
- required integrations
- data access scope
- supported environments
- human approval requirements
- expected outputs
- known limitations
- update history
- support contact

## Review & Trust

Marketplace governance can include:

- publisher identity
- technical review
- permission review
- security review
- quality evaluation
- versioning
- change review
- deprecation policy
- user ratings and feedback
- incident handling

## Private Enterprise Marketplace

Large organizations may operate their own private catalog of approved internal AI employees, workflows and skills.

This allows enterprise teams to standardize reusable automation while preserving internal governance.

## Business Models

Potential commercial models may include:

- free extensions
- paid one-time assets
- subscription agents
- usage-based capabilities
- enterprise licensing
- developer revenue share
- managed deployment services

## Ecosystem Flywheel

**More Builders → More Skills & Agents → More Use Cases → More Users → More Feedback → Better Ecosystem**

## Principle

**Extensibility should increase capability without weakening governance.**

# 中文

## 让智慧生命体拥有可扩展生态

成熟的 KING AI SEA 生态可以允许客户和开发者通过经过审核的 Agent、AI 员工、技能、工作流、集成、知识包和行业方案扩展智慧生命体。

Marketplace 不是让未经审核的自动化随意获得权限。每个扩展都必须清楚说明：它做什么、需要什么、能访问什么。

## Marketplace 分类

### AI 员工
客服、销售、市场、运营、IT、安全、财务、HR、管理辅助等。

### 行业 Agent
酒店、物流、物业、医疗行政、养老管理、房地产、零售、餐饮、教育、SaaS、制造、现场服务、旅游等。

### Skills
针对具体动作或工作流的可复用能力。

### Workflow Packs
围绕重复业务目标预先定义的流程模板。

### Integrations
连接外部工具、服务和企业系统的授权连接器。

### Knowledge Packs
面向行业或工作流的结构化公共知识或企业自有知识包。

### Website / App Components
可以嵌入网站、手机 App 和 SaaS 的智慧组件。

## Extension Manifest

高质量扩展应该明确：

- 名称
- 发布者
- 版本
- 用途
- 所需权限
- 所需集成
- 数据访问范围
- 支持环境
- 人工审批要求
- 预期输出
- 已知限制
- 更新历史
- 支持联系方式

## 审核与信任

Marketplace 治理可以包括：

- 发布者身份
- 技术审核
- 权限审核
- 安全审核
- 质量评估
- 版本管理
- 变更审核
- 下架/废弃策略
- 用户评分与反馈
- 事件处理

## 企业私有 Marketplace

大型企业可以建立自己的内部 Agent / Skill / Workflow 商店，只允许经过审核的企业内部扩展使用。

这样企业可以标准化复用自动化能力，同时保持内部治理。

## 商业模式

未来可以包括：

- 免费扩展
- 一次性付费资产
- 订阅制 Agent
- 按使用量收费
- 企业授权
- 开发者分成
- 托管部署服务

## 生态飞轮

**更多开发者 → 更多 Skills / Agents → 更多场景 → 更多用户 → 更多反馈 → 更强生态**

## 原则

**扩展能力越强，治理不能越弱。**
