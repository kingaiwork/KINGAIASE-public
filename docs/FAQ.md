# English

## What is KING AI SEA?

KING AI SEA is an intelligent lifeform system for individuals and enterprises. It is designed to go beyond one-off AI conversations by combining persistent context, goal understanding, coordinated assistance, authorized action, operational awareness, and continuous improvement into one long-term digital intelligence experience.

## Is KING AI SEA a chatbot?

No. Chat is one possible interface, but the product vision is broader. KING AI SEA is designed as a persistent intelligent layer that can understand goals, remember useful context, coordinate specialized AI roles, connect to approved tools and systems, assist with execution, surface important changes, and support long-running personal or business workflows.

## What does “intelligent lifeform” mean?

It describes the product experience: an AI system that can maintain identity and useful context over time, understand an operating environment, participate in recurring work, react to approved signals, learn from outcomes, and become more aligned with the person or organization using it. The term describes the user-facing system concept and does not disclose proprietary implementation details.

## Who is KING AI SEA for?

KING AI SEA is designed for founders, professionals, creators, developers, independent operators, small and medium businesses, and enterprises that want AI to become part of ongoing work rather than remain a separate question-and-answer tool.

## What can KING AI SEA do for individuals?

It can support personal knowledge, research, planning, project coordination, communication, digital operations, reminders, long-term goals, creative work, technical assistance, and approved cross-service workflows. The goal is to become a practical personal second brain and digital operating partner.

## What can KING AI SEA do for businesses?

It can support executive reporting, customer service, sales operations, marketing, internal knowledge, IT operations, security awareness, finance workflows, HR support, SOP execution, business monitoring, and multi-agent coordination. The goal is to help organizations build a controlled AI workforce around real business objectives.

## What is an AI workforce?

An AI workforce is a coordinated set of specialized AI roles working around business goals. For example, an organization may use different AI roles for customer service, sales, operations, technical systems, security, reporting, and management while maintaining one unified operating experience.

## Can KING AI SEA take actions?

With user-approved integrations and permissions, KING AI SEA can assist with actions across connected digital systems and workflows. The scope of automation, execution permissions, approval requirements, and security boundaries are defined by the user or organization.

## Can KING AI SEA remember information over time?

The product is designed around durable, useful context so that recurring work does not have to restart from zero. Public materials describe the capability and user experience only; proprietary memory architecture and implementation details are not disclosed.

## Does KING AI SEA support private deployment?

KING AI SEA is designed to support deployment models ranging from cloud environments and private VPS infrastructure to enterprise-controlled environments and hybrid architectures, depending on project requirements.

## Is KING AI SEA open source?

This repository is a public product and documentation repository. It is not a publication of KING AI SEA’s proprietary core technology. Internal orchestration, memory internals, security mechanisms, private prompts, model-routing logic, self-evolution implementation, privileged control mechanisms, credentials, and production infrastructure details are intentionally not disclosed.

## How is KING AI SEA different from a normal AI assistant?

A normal AI assistant is often optimized for answering a prompt. KING AI SEA is positioned around continuity and outcomes: understand the goal, preserve useful context, coordinate work, assist with authorized execution, observe results, and improve the operating relationship over time.

## Is KING AI SEA only for technical teams?

No. Technical operations are one use case. KING AI SEA is also designed for business operations, customer service, sales, marketing, management, research, personal productivity, knowledge work, and private digital assistance.

# 中文

## 什么是 KING AI 智慧生命体 SEA？

KING AI 智慧生命体 SEA 是面向个人与企业的智慧生命体系统。它的目标不是停留在一次性的 AI 对话，而是把长期上下文、目标理解、协同辅助、授权执行、运行感知与持续优化整合成一个能够长期参与真实工作与数字生活的智慧系统。

## KING AI SEA 是聊天机器人吗？

不是。聊天可以是交互入口之一，但 KING AI SEA 的定位远不止聊天。它希望成为一个持续存在的智慧层：理解目标、保留有价值的上下文、协调不同专业 AI 角色、连接经过授权的工具和系统、辅助执行任务、发现重要变化，并参与长期个人或企业工作流。

它描述的是产品体验：一个能够长期保持身份与有效上下文、理解自身运行环境、参与重复工作、响应经过授权的信号、从结果中积累经验，并越来越适应个人或企业工作方式的 AI 系统。这个概念用于描述对外产品能力，不公开专有核心实现。

## KING AI SEA 适合谁？

适合创业者、专业人士、创作者、开发者、独立经营者、中小企业和大型企业，尤其适合希望 AI 真正进入长期工作，而不仅仅作为问答工具使用的人和组织。
