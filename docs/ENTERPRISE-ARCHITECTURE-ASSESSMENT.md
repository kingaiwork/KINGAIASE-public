# English

## 1. Purpose

The assessment helps an organization answer five questions:

1. Where can persistent intelligence create meaningful value?
2. Which workflows should remain human-led, AI-assisted or AI-operated?
3. What systems and knowledge may be connected?
4. What permissions, approvals and boundaries are required?
5. What deployment and measurement model fits the organization?

## 2. Assessment Domains

### A. Business Goals
- Revenue growth
- Customer experience
- Cost and cycle-time reduction
- Operational resilience
- Employee productivity
- Knowledge access
- Digital transformation
- New intelligent products or services

### B. Candidate Missions
For each mission document:
- Business outcome
- Current process
- Frequency
- Current owner
- Systems involved
- Data involved
- Decision points
- Risk level
- Human approvals
- Success metric

### C. Workforce Design
Classify work into:
- Human-led
- Human + AI collaboration
- AI-assisted
- AI-operated with approval
- AI-operated within policy

Define candidate AI roles, their human owners and escalation paths.

### D. Knowledge Readiness
Inventory:
- SOPs
- Policies
- Product/service knowledge
- Customer information
- Internal documentation
- Operational data
- Historical decisions
- Reporting sources
- Approved external information

Evaluate freshness, ownership, sensitivity and access boundaries.

### E. Integration Readiness
Map approved systems such as:
- Email
- Calendar
- CRM
- ERP
- Help desk
- CMS
- Websites
- SaaS applications
- Databases
- Files
- APIs
- Cloud infrastructure
- Developer systems
- Internal applications

For each connection document read/write scope and human approval requirements.

### F. Trust & Governance
Define:
- Identity model
- Roles
- Permission scopes
- Approval thresholds
- Sensitive operations
- Escalation
- Audit requirements
- Retention requirements
- Incident handling
- Human override

### G. Deployment
Evaluate potential patterns:
- Shared cloud
- Dedicated environment
- Private VPS/server
- Enterprise private cloud
- Hybrid deployment
- Local/private model integration

Selection depends on verified technical requirements and available deployment options.

### H. Observability & Evaluation
Decide what leaders need to see:
- Mission state
- Task state
- Human approvals
- Failure and exception rate
- Quality score
- Completion rate
- Time saved
- Cost per outcome
- Customer impact
- Revenue influence
- Escalation frequency
- Security events

## 3. Mission Scoring Matrix

Score each candidate mission from 1–5:

| Dimension | Question |
|---|---|
| Business Value | Does successful execution materially matter? |
| Repetition | Does the mission occur frequently? |
| Knowledge Availability | Is required information accessible and governable? |
| Integration Readiness | Can relevant systems be connected safely? |
| Decision Complexity | Can decisions be bounded or escalated? |
| Risk | What happens if the result is wrong? |
| Measurability | Can outcomes be objectively evaluated? |
| Human Leverage | Does AI free valuable human capacity? |

High-value, measurable, bounded missions are usually better early candidates than high-risk ambiguous missions.

## 4. Adoption Stages

### Stage 1 — Observe & Assist
Read, summarize, search, draft and recommend.

### Stage 2 — Coordinate
Track missions, route tasks and maintain status visibility.

### Stage 3 — Act With Approval
Prepare or execute actions after explicit human approval.

### Stage 4 — Policy-Bounded Operation
Execute defined low-risk work within approved policy.

### Stage 5 — Intelligent Workforce
Multiple AI roles coordinate under a shared governance and control model.

### Stage 6 — Intelligent-Lifeform Operations
Persistent continuity, broad organizational context, governed action and controlled improvement operate as one system.

## 5. Assessment Deliverables

A production assessment can produce:
- Executive opportunity map
- Mission inventory
- AI Workforce role map
- Integration map
- Knowledge-readiness map
- Governance matrix
- Deployment recommendation
- KPI baseline
- Pilot design
- Risk register
- 30/60/90-day rollout plan

## 6. Public Boundary

This framework describes questions and deliverables only. It does not publish KING AI SEA internal architecture, security logic, memory implementation, orchestration, model routing, prompts or self-evolution internals.

# 中文

## 1. 评估目的

企业架构评估用于回答五个核心问题：

1. 哪些业务最值得引入持续型智慧？
2. 哪些工作应该保持人工主导、AI 辅助或在规则内由 AI 执行？
3. 哪些系统和知识可以连接？
4. 权限、审批和边界应该如何定义？
5. 企业适合什么部署与衡量方式？

## 2. 八大评估域

### A. 业务目标
收入、客户体验、成本、效率、运营韧性、员工生产力、知识利用、数字化、新产品。

### B. Mission 候选库
每个 Mission 记录：业务结果、现有流程、频率、负责人、涉及系统、涉及数据、决策节点、风险等级、人工审批和成功指标。

### C. AI Workforce 设计
将工作分为：
- 人工主导
- 人类 + AI 协作
- AI 辅助
- AI 执行但需要审批
- AI 在政策边界内执行

并为每个 AI 岗位定义人类负责人和升级路径。

### D. 知识准备度
盘点 SOP、制度、产品知识、客户资料、内部文档、运营数据、历史决策、报表数据以及经过授权的外部信息。

### E. 集成准备度
评估 Email、Calendar、CRM、ERP、Help Desk、CMS、网站、SaaS、数据库、文件、API、Cloud、开发系统和内部应用。

### F. 信任与治理
定义身份、角色、权限、审批阈值、敏感操作、升级机制、审计、保留策略、事件处理和人工覆盖权。

### G. 部署
根据真实需求评估 Cloud、Dedicated、Private VPS/Server、Enterprise Private Cloud、Hybrid 和本地模型集成。

### H. 可观测与评估
明确管理层需要看到的任务状态、审批、失败率、质量、完成率、节省时间、单位结果成本、客户影响、收入影响、升级次数和安全事件。

## 3. Mission 评分

建议对业务价值、重复频率、知识可用性、集成准备度、决策复杂度、风险、可衡量性和人类杠杆效应进行 1–5 分评估。

早期更适合选择：**价值高、可衡量、边界清楚、风险可控** 的 Mission。

## 4. 企业采用阶段

**Stage 1 观察与辅助 → Stage 2 协调 → Stage 3 审批后行动 → Stage 4 策略边界内执行 → Stage 5 AI Workforce → Stage 6 智慧生命体运营。**

## 5. 评估交付物

可以形成：
- 高管机会地图
- Mission 清单
- AI Workforce 岗位图
- 集成地图
- 知识准备度
- 治理矩阵
- 部署建议
- KPI 基线
- Pilot 设计
- 风险清单
- 30/60/90 天路线图

## 6. 技术保护

公开评估框架只展示需要解决的问题和交付成果，不公开 KING AI SEA 的内部架构、Memory 实现、模型路由、安全机制、Prompt、Agent Orchestration 或自进化核心技术。
