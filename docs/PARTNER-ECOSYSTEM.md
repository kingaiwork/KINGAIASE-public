# English

## 1. Why an Ecosystem Matters

A persistent intelligent-lifeform platform cannot create maximum value in isolation.

Organizations already operate through websites, applications, cloud infrastructure, databases, communication channels, CRM, ERP, industry systems, developer platforms and service partners.

The KING AI SEA ecosystem is therefore designed around a simple idea:

### **One intelligent lifeform. Many trusted ways to connect, build, deploy and specialize.**

## 2. Partner Categories

### Technology Partners
Platforms, infrastructure providers, model providers, data systems, communication services, developer tools and other technologies that can be connected through approved integrations.

### Integration Partners
Teams that help connect KING AI SEA with existing business systems, APIs, data, websites and applications.

### Solution Partners
Organizations that design complete AI Workforce, intelligent website, intelligent application or automation solutions around customer outcomes.

### Industry Partners
Domain specialists that contribute industry workflows, terminology, operating knowledge and deployment requirements.

### Developer Partners
Builders creating agents, skills, workflows, integrations, embedded experiences and future marketplace components.

### Deployment Partners
Infrastructure and managed-service providers that help customers operate cloud, VPS, private or hybrid deployments.

### Security & Governance Partners
Specialists supporting security review, governance design, enterprise policy, audit readiness and risk management.

### Education & Enablement Partners
Organizations helping customers, developers and operators learn how to design, govern and operate AI Workforce systems.

### Referral & Commercial Partners
Approved organizations that introduce customers, support local markets or participate in commercial expansion.

## 5. Partner Design Principles

### Customer Outcome First
Partner work should map to a measurable customer mission rather than adding technology for its own sake.

### One Governed Intelligence Experience
Integrations should strengthen the unified KING AI SEA experience rather than create fragmented, unmanaged agents.

### Explicit Permission
External systems and tools should be connected only through approved authorization and policy.

### Clear Ownership
Every production workflow should have identifiable technical and business ownership.

### Observability
Customers should be able to understand what is running, what is failing, what is waiting for approval and what value is being produced.

### Protected Core
Partners can extend public surfaces and approved interfaces without receiving proprietary internal algorithms, prompts, evolution mechanisms or privileged security logic unless separately authorized under appropriate agreements.

## 6. Partner Journey

### Discover
Identify a customer problem, industry opportunity or technology integration.

### Design
Define mission, users, systems, permissions, workflow and measurable outcome.

### Validate
Test the solution in a controlled environment with realistic scenarios.

### Deploy
Move into an approved cloud, VPS, private or hybrid environment.

### Operate
Monitor quality, incidents, customer results and changing business requirements.

### Expand
Extend the solution into additional AI roles, workflows, systems or markets.

# 中文

## 1. 为什么智慧生命体需要生态

一个长期智慧生命体平台不可能在完全孤立的环境中创造最大价值。

企业本来就运行在网站、APP、Cloud、数据库、通信渠道、CRM、ERP、行业系统、开发平台和各种服务合作伙伴组成的数字世界中。

因此 KING AI SEA 的生态理念是：

### **一个智慧生命体，多种可信的连接、构建、部署与行业化方式。**

## 2. 合作伙伴类型

### 技术合作伙伴
包括基础设施、模型、数据平台、通信服务、开发工具以及其他可以通过授权集成连接的技术体系。

### 集成合作伙伴
帮助 KING AI SEA 与企业现有 API、业务系统、数据、网站和 APP 对接。

### 解决方案合作伙伴
围绕客户业务结果设计完整 AI Workforce、智慧网站、智慧 APP 和自动化方案。

### 行业合作伙伴
提供行业流程、术语、运营知识和部署要求的垂直领域专家。

### 开发者合作伙伴
开发 Agent、Skill、Workflow、Integration、嵌入式体验以及未来 Marketplace 组件。

### 部署合作伙伴
帮助客户运营 Cloud、VPS、Private 或 Hybrid 环境。

### 安全与治理合作伙伴
支持安全评估、治理设计、企业策略、审计准备和风险管理。

### 教育与赋能合作伙伴
帮助客户、开发者和运营团队学习如何设计、治理和运行 AI Workforce。

### 推荐与商业合作伙伴
在经过批准后进行客户推荐、本地市场拓展或商业合作。

## 5. 生态设计原则

### 业务结果优先
合作方案应该服务于明确客户目标，而不是为了增加技术而增加技术。

### 一个统一受治理的智慧体验
集成应该强化 KING AI SEA 的统一体验，而不是制造大量无法统一管理的孤立 Agent。

### 明确授权
外部系统和 Tool 只能在授权与策略允许范围内连接。

### 明确责任
每一个生产工作流都应该有清楚的技术和业务负责人。

### 可观测
客户应该知道什么正在运行、什么失败、什么等待审批、创造了什么价值。

### 核心技术保护
合作伙伴可以在公开接口和授权扩展面上进行建设，但核心算法、私有 Prompt、进化机制和高权限安全逻辑继续受到保护，除非在适当协议下另行授权。
