# English

## 1. ROI Starts With the Mission

Do not calculate ROI for “AI” as one abstract category. Calculate it for a defined mission.

A mission should have:
- A business owner
- A current process
- A measurable baseline
- A defined AI role
- A permission model
- A success metric
- A review period

## 2. Five Sources of Value

### A. Labor Leverage
Estimated value from reducing repetitive human effort while preserving human responsibility where required.

**Annual Labor Leverage = Hours Saved per Period × Periods per Year × Loaded Hourly Cost**

### B. Cycle-Time Reduction
Value created by faster response, faster processing, faster follow-up or shorter operational cycles.

Examples:
- Faster lead response
- Faster quote preparation
- Faster customer resolution
- Faster invoice processing
- Faster report preparation

### C. Revenue Influence
Potential value from better coverage and follow-up.

Examples:
- More qualified leads contacted
- Fewer missed inquiries
- Faster follow-up
- Improved booking completion
- More renewal opportunities surfaced

Only count measured incremental impact, not all revenue touched by AI.

### D. Quality & Error Reduction
Potential value from fewer omissions, lower rework, better consistency and earlier exception detection.

### E. Risk & Visibility
Potential value from earlier detection, better auditability, policy enforcement, human escalation and management visibility.

Risk value should be evaluated carefully and not converted into exaggerated savings without evidence.

## 3. Cost Model

Include:
- Platform subscription/license
- Model/compute usage
- Integration work
- Deployment/infrastructure
- Monitoring and evaluation
- Human review/approval time
- Change management
- Training and documentation
- Ongoing support

## 4. Core Formulas

### Net Annual Value
**Net Annual Value = Measured Annual Benefits − Annual Operating Cost**

### ROI
**ROI % = (Measured Benefits − Total Cost) / Total Cost × 100**

### Payback Period
**Payback Period = Initial Investment / Average Monthly Net Benefit**

These formulas are simple financial planning tools, not guarantees.

## 5. Mission ROI Worksheet

| Field | Example Input |
|---|---|
| Mission | Customer inquiry triage |
| Monthly volume | Actual measured volume |
| Current handling time | Actual baseline |
| Human loaded cost | Company estimate |
| Target AI assistance | Defined workflow scope |
| Human review time | Pilot measurement |
| Error/rework baseline | Actual measurement |
| Revenue influence | Only verified incremental effect |
| Platform/compute cost | Actual deployment estimate |
| Integration cost | Actual project estimate |

## 6. Value Tiers

### Tier 1 — Efficiency
Time saved, fewer manual steps, faster processing.

### Tier 2 — Service Quality
Faster responses, higher coverage, better consistency.

### Tier 3 — Revenue Enablement
More leads followed up, faster sales cycles, better retention workflows.

### Tier 4 — Operating Intelligence
Better visibility, exception detection, management coordination and decision support.

### Tier 5 — New Business Model
New intelligent products, AI-native services, AI-powered websites/apps, digital workforce offerings.

## 7. Pilot Recommendation

For early pilots, choose a mission that is:
- Frequent
- Measurable
- Valuable
- Bounded
- Supported by available knowledge
- Connected to accessible systems
- Low-to-moderate risk

Measure baseline for at least one meaningful business period before making ROI claims.

## 8. Public Integrity Rule

KING AI public materials should not publish invented ROI percentages, guaranteed cost savings, guaranteed revenue increases or unsupported productivity claims.

Case studies should distinguish:
- Observed
- Measured
- Estimated
- Projected

# 中文

## 1. ROI 要从 Mission 算，不要从“AI”算

企业不应该问“AI 能带来多少 ROI”，而应该问：

**某一个明确业务 Mission，在引入 KING AI SEA 后，真实结果发生了什么变化？**

每个 Mission 都要有负责人、现有流程、真实基线、AI 岗位、权限模型、成功指标和评估周期。

## 2. 五类价值来源

### A. 人力杠杆
通过减少重复工作释放人类时间。

**年度人力价值 = 每周期节省工时 × 年度周期数 × 综合小时成本**

### B. 周期缩短
例如更快响应线索、更快报价、更快客服处理、更快发票、更快报表。

### C. 收入影响
例如减少漏掉的询盘、提高跟进覆盖、缩短销售周期、提高预约完成、发现续费/增购机会。

只能计算经过验证的增量影响，不能把所有 AI 接触过的收入都算成 AI 创造。

### D. 质量与错误减少
返工减少、遗漏减少、一致性提高、异常更早发现。

### E. 风险与可见性
更早发现风险、审计更完整、策略执行更清晰、人工升级更及时、管理层更容易看到问题。

## 3. 成本必须算完整

包括平台费用、模型/算力、集成、部署、基础设施、监控评估、人工审批、变更管理、培训、文档和持续支持。

## 4. 三个基础公式

**年度净价值 = 年度可测收益 − 年度运行成本**

**ROI =（可测收益 − 总成本）/ 总成本 × 100%**

**回收期 = 初始投资 / 平均月度净收益**

这些只是财务规划方法，不是收益保证。

## 5. 五级价值

**Tier 1 效率 → Tier 2 服务质量 → Tier 3 收入赋能 → Tier 4 运营智慧 → Tier 5 新商业模式。**

KING AI SEA 的长期价值不应该只停留在“节省几个人工小时”，而应该逐渐进入运营智慧、数字劳动力和新的 AI-Native 产品/服务。

## 6. Pilot 选择原则

优先选择：高频、可衡量、有价值、边界清楚、知识可用、系统可连接、风险可控的 Mission。

## 7. 公开诚信规则

KING AI 的公开材料不应虚构 ROI、节省比例、收入增长或生产力数字。

所有案例必须清楚区分：

**Observed（观察到） / Measured（测量到） / Estimated（估算） / Projected（预测）**。
