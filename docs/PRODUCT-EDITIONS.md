# English

## One System, Multiple Editions

KING AI SEA remains one unified intelligent-lifeform system. Editions represent different operating scopes, deployment levels, governance needs and integration depth — not separate underlying products.

## Personal

For individuals who want a persistent second brain and digital operating partner.

Typical capabilities:

- personal long-term context
- planning and reminders
- email and communication assistance
- research and knowledge
- personal projects
- content and creativity
- technical assistance
- proactive notifications
- approved app/service integrations

Best for:

- individuals
- professionals
- students
- creators
- knowledge workers

## Professional

For founders, consultants, freelancers, creators and independent operators who need one intelligence layer across work and personal business operations.

Adds emphasis on:

- business communication
- customer follow-up
- content and marketing
- project operations
- website operations
- reporting
- technical workflows
- recurring business reviews

## Business

For small and medium teams that want AI employees and workflow automation.

Typical capabilities:

- multiple AI employee roles
- customer service
- sales assistance
- marketing operations
- business knowledge
- workflow automation
- recurring reporting
- website agent
- team permissions
- approval workflows
- system integrations

## Enterprise

For organizations that need deeper governance, private deployment, complex integrations and coordinated AI workforce operations.

Typical capabilities:

- enterprise AI workforce
- department-specific AI employees
- role and permission governance
- human-in-the-loop approvals
- private or hybrid deployment
- enterprise knowledge connections
- custom integrations
- auditability
- observability
- evaluation
- long-running workflows
- multi-location operations
- multilingual operations
- custom policy and escalation structures

## Developer

For builders, SaaS companies, agencies and internal platform teams.

Typical capabilities:

- agent creation
- workflow building
- skills and tools
- APIs and SDKs
- application embedding
- custom integrations
- evaluation
- observability
- deployment templates
- agent interoperability

## Industry

For organizations that require deeply tailored roles, terminology, workflows and operating rules.

Examples:

- hospitality
- logistics
- property management
- senior-care administration
- healthcare administration
- professional services
- real estate
- retail
- restaurants
- education
- SaaS
- manufacturing
- field services
- travel

## Website Intelligence

For public-facing organizations that want a 24/7 intelligent digital front door.

Typical capabilities:

- AI receptionist
- customer service
- intelligent site search
- lead qualification
- booking
- quote intake
- product/service discovery
- multilingual support
- human escalation
- follow-up workflows

## Application Intelligence

For software products and internal applications that want natural-language operation and agentic workflows.

Typical capabilities:

- embedded copilot
- domain agent
- AI support
- workflow generation
- natural-language analytics
- action-taking assistant
- voice interaction
- intelligent onboarding

## Private Intelligence

For deployments where the environment, integrations, data boundaries and governance need stronger operator control.

Potential environments:

- dedicated VPS
- private cloud
- enterprise infrastructure
- hybrid environment
- isolated business environments

## Product Expansion Model

A customer can begin with one narrow, high-value workflow and expand over time:

**Single Agent → AI Employee → Department AI → AI Workforce → Enterprise Intelligent Lifeform**

or for an individual:

**Assistant → Second Brain → Personal Operator → Persistent Digital Intelligence**

## Commercial Principle

Do not sell “more AI” as the value proposition.

Sell measurable outcomes such as:

- faster response
- less repetitive work
- fewer missed follow-ups
- better information access
- better workflow consistency
- improved operating visibility
- lower coordination burden
- faster execution
- better customer experience

## 一个系统，多种产品形态

KING AI SEA 始终是一套统一智慧生命体系统。不同版本代表不同的使用范围、部署等级、治理要求和集成深度，并不是底层彼此独立的产品。

## Personal 个人版

面向希望拥有长期第二大脑和数字伙伴的个人。

典型能力：

- 个人长期上下文
- 日程、计划与提醒
- 邮件和沟通辅助
- 研究与知识
- 个人项目
- 内容与创作
- 技术辅助
- 主动提醒
- 经授权的 App/服务连接

适合：个人、专业人士、学生、创作者、知识工作者。

## Professional 专业版

面向创业者、顾问、自由职业者、创作者和独立经营者，让一个智慧层覆盖个人工作和小型业务运营。

重点增加：

- 商务沟通
- 客户跟进
- 内容和市场
- 项目运营
- 网站运营
- 报表
- 技术流程
- 周期经营复盘

## Business 企业团队版

面向需要 AI 员工和工作流自动化的中小团队。

典型能力：

- 市场运营
- 企业知识

- 更快响应
- 更少重复劳动
- 更少漏掉的跟进
- 更好的信息访问
- 更稳定的工作流
- 更清楚的运营可见性
- 更低的协调成本
- 更快执行
- 更好的客户体验
