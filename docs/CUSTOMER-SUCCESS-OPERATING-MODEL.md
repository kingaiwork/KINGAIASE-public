# 🤝 KING AI SEA — Customer Success Operating Model

# English

## Success Stages
1. outcome alignment
2. onboarding
3. integration readiness
4. pilot adoption
5. KPI review
6. production stabilization
7. expansion
8. quarterly architecture/business review

## Success Signals
- mission adoption
- successful outcomes
- declining rework/escalation where appropriate
- trusted human handoff
- stable integrations
- acceptable cost
- user confidence
- expansion based on evidence

## Health Review
Track business, product, technical, trust and support health separately.

# 中文
客户成功阶段：结果对齐 → 入职 → 集成就绪 → Pilot 采用 → KPI 复核 → 生产稳定 → 扩展 → 季度架构/业务复盘。

健康信号包括 Mission 使用、成功结果、合理下降的返工/升级、可信人工交接、稳定集成、可接受成本、用户信心和基于证据的扩展。

业务、产品、技术、信任和支持健康度应分别观察。
