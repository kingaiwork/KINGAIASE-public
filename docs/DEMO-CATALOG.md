# English

## Demo 01 — Personal Second Brain

Show a user returning after weeks and asking:

> “What were the three most important things I was working on, what changed, and what should I do next?”

Demonstrate continuity, project context, priorities, reminders, documents and next-action support.

## Demo 02 — AI Chief of Staff

Show a business owner opening one morning briefing containing:

- revenue/customer signals
- project blockers
- important emails
- approvals waiting
- system issues
- decisions needed
- suggested priorities

## Demo 03 — AI Website Receptionist

A visitor arrives on a company website and says:

> “I need an AI system for my business but I don’t know where to start.”

The website agent understands the need, explains relevant options, asks useful questions, qualifies the lead, prepares an architecture-review request and routes it to the correct human team.

## Demo 04 — AI Customer Service Employee

A customer asks a support question. The AI employee retrieves approved product knowledge, understands account context, proposes a resolution, creates a support case if needed and escalates when the issue requires a human.

## Demo 05 — AI Sales Employee

Show a new lead being researched, qualified, summarized, routed, followed up and converted into a structured CRM opportunity.

## Demo 06 — AI Workforce

One business objective is delegated across multiple roles:

**Executive → Research → Marketing → Sales → Operations → Reporting**

Show coordination without forcing the user to manage every agent separately.

## Demo 07 — Long-Running Workflow

A multi-day task remains active across time, preserving state, waiting for an external event, requesting approval and continuing after approval.

## Demo 08 — Enterprise Knowledge Intelligence

An employee asks:

> “Why did we make this decision last quarter, which policy applies, and what changed since then?”

Show retrieval across approved sources with clear context and references.

## Demo 09 — AI Operations Control Center

Show a dashboard with:

- agent status
- current missions
- approval queue
- workflow failures
- cost
- quality
- security alerts
- human escalations

## Demo 10 — AI Website + Back Office

A website request becomes a real internal workflow:

**Visitor conversation → qualified lead → CRM → sales task → follow-up → management visibility**

## Demo 11 — Intelligent SaaS

Inside an application, the user says:

> “Find unpaid invoices older than 30 days, summarize risk, and prepare follow-up drafts.”

The application converts the goal into a structured workflow instead of requiring manual navigation.

## Demo 12 — Voice AI Receptionist

A caller speaks naturally, receives answers, books a service, and is transferred to a human when the request requires escalation.

## Demo 13 — Multimodal Workflow

**Voice request → upload image/document → understand content → create structured task → human approval → execute → report**

## Demo 14 — AI DevOps / System Operations

Show an approved system environment being checked, logs summarized, a likely cause identified, a safe recovery proposal prepared, approval requested and the outcome documented.

## Demo 15 — Security & Governance

Show the system refusing to bypass a configured boundary and instead routing a sensitive action into an approval workflow.

## Demo 16 — Industry Agent

Demonstrate one vertical workflow end-to-end, such as hotel guest operations, logistics exception handling, property maintenance intake or senior-care administration.

## Demo 17 — Agent Marketplace

Show a user discovering an approved AI employee or workflow pack, reviewing permissions, installing it into a controlled environment and seeing it appear in the Control Center.

## Demo 18 — Agent Commerce

A customer asks for a service, receives an option, prepares a booking or quote, and reaches the final authorization step with explicit user control.

## Demo Design Rule

Every flagship demo should communicate five things:

1. a real human/business goal
2. useful intelligence
3. action or workflow
4. control and visibility
5. measurable outcome

Avoid demos that are only impressive conversations with no operational result.

# 中文

## Demo 01 — 个人第二大脑

用户几周后回来问：

> “我之前最重要的三件事情是什么？现在发生了什么变化？我下一步应该做什么？”

展示长期连续性、项目背景、优先级、提醒、文档和下一步辅助。

## Demo 02 — AI Chief of Staff

企业负责人早上打开一份统一简报：

- 收入/客户信号
- 项目阻塞
- 重要邮件
- 待审批
- 系统问题
- 需要决策的事项
- 建议优先级

## Demo 03 — AI 网站前台

访客进入企业官网说：

> “我想给公司部署 AI，但是不知道从哪里开始。”

网站 Agent 理解需求、介绍适合方案、提出关键问题、判断线索、生成架构评估请求并转给正确的人类团队。

## Demo 04 — AI 客服员工

客户提出问题，AI 员工读取授权产品知识、理解客户背景、提出解决方案，需要时创建工单，并在必须人工处理时升级。

## Demo 05 — AI 销售员工

展示新线索从研究、判断、摘要、分配、跟进一直进入 CRM 商机的全过程。

## Demo 06 — AI Workforce

一个业务目标由多个角色共同完成：

**管理 → 研究 → 市场 → 销售 → 运营 → 汇报**

用户不需要自己管理每个 Agent。

## Demo 07 — 长时间 Workflow

展示一个跨多天运行的任务：保留状态、等待外部事件、请求审批，并在批准后继续执行。

## Demo 08 — 企业知识智慧

员工问：

> “我们上季度为什么做这个决定？适用哪个制度？后来发生了什么变化？”

从经过授权的信息源中恢复历史背景和相关来源。

## Demo 09 — AI Control Center

展示统一看板：

- Agent 状态
- 当前 Mission
- 审批队列
- Workflow 失败
- 成本
- 质量
- 安全告警
- 人工升级

## Demo 10 — AI 官网连接企业后台

展示：

**访客对话 → 合格线索 → CRM → 销售任务 → 后续跟进 → 管理层可见**

## Demo 11 — 智慧 SaaS

用户在软件中直接说：

> “找到超过 30 天未付款的发票，整理风险并准备跟进内容。”

系统把目标转成结构化工作流，而不是要求用户自己操作大量页面。

## Demo 12 — AI 电话前台

客户自然说话，AI 完成回答、预约，并在需要人工时完成转接。

## Demo 13 — 多模态 Workflow

**语音提出目标 → 上传图片/文档 → 理解 → 创建结构化任务 → 人工审批 → 执行 → 汇报**

## Demo 14 — AI DevOps / 系统运维

展示授权环境检查、日志摘要、可能原因判断、安全恢复建议、审批以及结果记录。

## Demo 15 — 安全与治理

展示系统面对超出权限的敏感动作时不会越权，而是自动进入审批流程。

## Demo 16 — 行业 Agent

完整展示一个行业工作流，例如酒店客户运营、物流异常、物业维修请求或养老行政管理。

## Demo 17 — Agent Marketplace

用户找到经过审核的 AI 员工/Workflow，查看权限要求，安装到受控环境，并在 Control Center 中统一管理。

## Demo 18 — Agent Commerce

客户提出服务需求，AI 帮助选择、准备预约或报价，并最终在明确用户授权下完成关键商业步骤。

## Demo 设计原则

每个旗舰 Demo 必须让人看到五件事：

1. 真实目标
2. 有价值的智慧
3. 行动或工作流
4. 控制与可见性
5. 可衡量结果

不要只做“聊天很厉害但没有实际结果”的 Demo。
