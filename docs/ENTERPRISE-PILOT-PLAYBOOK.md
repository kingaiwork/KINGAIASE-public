# English

## Goal

Turn an AI idea into one measurable, governed production candidate without attempting to transform the entire company at once.

## Before Day 0 — Select the Mission

Choose a mission that is:
- frequent
- measurable
- valuable
- bounded
- supported by usable knowledge
- connected to accessible systems
- low-to-moderate risk

Define:
- executive sponsor
- business owner
- human operator/owner
- AI role
- data scope
- integration scope
- approval rules
- baseline metrics
- success criteria

## Days 1–30 — Observe, Assist, Measure

### Objectives
- Understand the real process
- Establish baseline
- Connect approved knowledge
- Validate answer/task quality
- Keep humans in direct control

### Typical capabilities
- Search and summarize
- Draft responses
- Prepare reports
- Triage requests
- Recommend next actions
- Maintain mission status

### Deliverables
- Baseline scorecard
- Knowledge map
- Workflow map
- Risk register
- Evaluation set
- Human-feedback loop

### Exit criteria
Do not expand execution authority until quality, scope and exceptions are understood.

## Days 31–60 — Coordinate and Act With Approval

### Objectives
- Connect approved systems
- Introduce task/workflow coordination
- Test human approval checkpoints
- Measure cycle-time and workload impact

### Typical capabilities
- Prepare CRM/email/system updates
- Route tasks
- Schedule approved follow-up
- Create drafts/actions for approval
- Coordinate multiple AI roles where useful
- Track failures and escalations

### Deliverables
- Integration map
- Permission matrix
- Approval matrix
- KPI dashboard
- Exception playbook
- Pilot ROI estimate based on measured data

## Days 61–90 — Policy-Bounded Operations

### Objectives
- Automate clearly bounded low-risk work where justified
- Expand observability and governance
- Validate repeatability
- Decide whether to scale

### Typical capabilities
- Execute approved routine actions
- Run scheduled or event-driven missions
- Coordinate AI Workforce roles
- Produce executive operating summaries
- Monitor outcomes and exceptions

### Deliverables
- Production-candidate architecture
- Final pilot scorecard
- Security/governance review
- ROI assessment
- Scale recommendation
- Next-department opportunity map

## Scale Decision

Scale only when:
- business value is demonstrated
- error/exception behavior is understood
- human ownership is clear
- permissions are bounded
- integrations are stable enough
- quality is measurable
- operating cost is understood
- governance is accepted

## Scale Paths

1. Expand volume within the same mission.
2. Add adjacent missions in the same department.
3. Add additional AI employee roles.
4. Expand to a second department.
5. Introduce AI Control Center governance.
6. Move toward enterprise-wide intelligent-lifeform operations.

## Public Integrity

This playbook does not promise that every pilot can reach policy-bounded autonomous operation in 90 days. The 30/60/90 structure is an assessment and rollout framework, not a guaranteed schedule or outcome.

# 中文

## 目标

不要一开始就“AI 改造整个公司”。先把一个明确业务 Mission 做成**可衡量、可治理、可进入生产候选**的成功样板。

## Day 0 前：选择 Mission

优先选择：高频、可衡量、有价值、边界清楚、知识可用、系统可连接、风险较低或中等的工作。

明确：高管 Sponsor、业务负责人、人类岗位负责人、AI 岗位、数据范围、集成范围、审批规则、基线指标和成功标准。

## 1–30 天：观察、辅助、测量

重点先理解真实流程和建立基线。

典型能力：搜索总结、起草回复、准备报表、请求分流、下一步建议、Mission 状态维护。

交付：基线指标、知识地图、流程地图、风险清单、评估集、人类反馈机制。

**在质量、范围和异常没有看清楚之前，不扩大执行权限。**

## 31–60 天：协同 + 审批后行动

开始连接授权系统、建立任务协同和人工审批检查点。

典型能力：准备 CRM/邮件/系统动作、任务分配、跟进、审批后执行、多个 AI 岗位协作、失败/升级跟踪。

交付：集成地图、权限矩阵、审批矩阵、KPI 看板、异常处理、基于真实数据的 Pilot ROI 估算。

## 61–90 天：政策边界内运营

对于验证充分、风险较低的重复工作，可以逐步进入策略边界内执行，并强化可观测与治理。

交付：生产候选架构、最终 Pilot Scorecard、安全治理复核、ROI 评估、规模化建议、下一部门机会地图。

## 扩大部署条件

只有当业务价值已经证明、错误与异常清楚、负责人明确、权限边界明确、集成稳定、质量可衡量、成本清楚、治理被接受时再扩大。

## 扩大路径

同 Mission 扩量 → 同部门增加 Mission → 增加 AI 员工 → 第二部门 → Control Center → 企业级智慧生命体运营。

## 真实性说明

30/60/90 是评估和实施框架，不保证所有项目一定可以在 90 天内进入高自动化阶段，也不保证特定商业结果。
