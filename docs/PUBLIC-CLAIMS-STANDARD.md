# 📣 KING AI SEA — Public Claims Standard

# English
Public messaging must be ambitious but verifiable.

## Allowed
- explain intended capabilities and product architecture
- state real available features with current evidence
- describe roadmap as roadmap
- publish measured results with methodology and customer permission
- explain proprietary architecture at a high level

## Not Allowed Without Evidence
- “world's best / smartest / safest” as a factual ranking
- invented customer counts, revenue, partners or certifications
- benchmark percentages without test method/date/environment
- “fully autonomous” when approvals or human ownership remain
- “compliant with every law”
- “available” for roadmap-only functions

## Preferred Positioning
Use terms such as **flagship-grade**, **frontier intelligent-lifeform vision**, **advanced governed AI Workforce architecture**, and **designed for persistent intelligence** when factual superlative ranking is unavailable.

# 中文
公开文案必须有野心，但必须可验证。

可以：介绍能力、公开架构、真实已可用功能、明确标记路线图、发布有方法和授权的实测结果。

没有证据时禁止把“全球最聪明/最安全/第一”写成事实排名；禁止虚构客户、收入、伙伴、认证、Benchmark；禁止把规划中写成已上线；禁止无证据声称“符合所有法律”。

建议使用：**全球旗舰级愿景、前沿智慧生命体架构、先进受治理 AI Workforce、持续型数字智慧**等可准确表达目标的语言。
