# KING AI Unified Roadmap

## English

This roadmap describes direction, not guaranteed release dates. Product status must remain aligned with `PRODUCT-MATRIX.md`.

## Phase 1 — Public foundation and identity

Focus:
- Unified KING AI public positioning
- Global website and public project map
- KING AI Intelligence and public research
- Online Tools ecosystem
- No-payment account foundation
- Human verification and verified email
- Canonical SEO/GEO/AI-search sources
- Public trust and enterprise documentation

Success condition: users, search engines, enterprises and partners can clearly understand the ecosystem and enter through a low-friction public experience.

## Phase 2 — Persistent intelligence

Focus:
- Personal Intelligence
- Long-term useful continuity
- Knowledge and memory workflows
- Mission planning
- Research synthesis
- Context-aware assistance
- Enterprise knowledge systems

Success condition: KING AI becomes useful across repeated interactions and real workflows rather than isolated prompts.

## Phase 3 — AI Workforce and enterprise operations

Focus:
- Specialized AI roles
- Shared enterprise knowledge
- Mission libraries
- Human review points
- KPI and evaluation frameworks
- Website/App AI
- Managed enterprise workflows

Success condition: repeatable, measurable enterprise workflows can be delivered under defined scope and governance.

## Phase 4 — Controlled execution

Focus:
- KINGAIBOT maturity
- Durable task execution
- Policy-aware tool use
- MCP / A2A interoperability
- Approved API/workflow execution
- Better observability and recovery
- Safe deployment/update patterns

Success condition: authorized digital work can move from recommendation to controlled execution with reliable audit and recovery.

## Phase 5 — Developer platform

Focus:
- Stable public APIs
- SDKs and integration tooling
- Embedded intelligence
- Managed connectors
- Agent interoperability
- Verified intelligence packages
- Developer documentation and examples

Success condition: third-party developers can integrate KING AI capabilities without depending on private implementation details.

## Phase 6 — KINGAI OS and edge intelligence

Focus:
- Server foundation
- Desktop experience
- Local-first intelligence
- Model-neutral routing
- Agent capabilities and policy
- IoT / Edge profiles
- Secure update and release system

Success condition: intelligent-agent capabilities can operate as a governed computing layer across server, desktop and edge environments.

## Phase 7 — Ecosystem expansion

Long-term direction:
- Partner ecosystem
- Vertical solution network
- Embedded/OEM programs
- Agent marketplace concepts
- Agent commerce research
- Broader controlled evolution
- Distributed intelligent systems

These items remain Planned or Vision until separately validated.

## Cross-cutting requirements

Every phase should preserve:

- truthfulness of public status
- least privilege
- human authority
- model neutrality
- cloud neutrality where practical
- privacy-aware memory
- public/private source separation
- auditable and reversible change
- no fabricated product, customer or performance claims

# 中文

## KING AI 统一长期路线图

本路线图描述方向，不代表固定发布日期。所有功能是否“已可用”必须以 `PRODUCT-MATRIX.md` 为准。

### 第一阶段：公共基础与身份
统一品牌、全球官网、项目地图、Intelligence、Online Tools、无支付账户、人机验证、邮箱验证、SEO/GEO/AI Search、Trust 与企业公开资料。

### 第二阶段：持续智慧
个人智慧、长期连续性、知识与记忆、Mission 规划、研究综合、企业知识系统。

### 第三阶段：AI Workforce
专业 AI 岗位、共享知识、Mission 库、人工复核、KPI、Website/App AI 和企业持续工作流。

### 第四阶段：受控执行
持续增强 KINGAIBOT，推进 Durable Task、策略化工具、MCP/A2A、授权 API/工作流执行、可观察性、恢复和安全升级。

### 第五阶段：Developer Platform
稳定 API、SDK、连接器、Embedded Intelligence、互操作、可信情报包和开发者生态。

### 第六阶段：KINGAI OS / Edge
Server、Desktop、本地优先智慧、模型中立、Agent 权限、IoT/Edge、安全发布更新。

### 第七阶段：生态扩展
合作伙伴、行业网络、OEM、Marketplace、Agent Commerce、受控进化和分布式智慧系统。未独立验证前一律保持 Planned / Vision。
