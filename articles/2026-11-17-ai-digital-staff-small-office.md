# AI Digital Staff for a Small Office: Which Tasks to Hand Over First, and Which to Never Hand Over

> **TL;DR** — A small office gets good value from AI digital staff when the first tasks are repetitive, rule-based, and easy to check. Good starting points include sorting inquiries, drafting routine replies, preparing summaries, and assembling weekly reports from source files. Tasks involving money movement, formal commitments, sensitive personnel matters, and upset customers should stay with named people. Start in draft mode and widen the scope only after checks hold up.

An AI digital staff member is software that carries a defined office job under human supervision. It reads incoming material, follows a written procedure, produces a draft or a completed routine step, and records what it did. A vague instruction such as "handle the inbox" gives the system too much room and gives the owner too little to check. Offices that start well start narrow: one job, a clear input, a clear output, and a person who can spot errors quickly.

## Start with work that is easy to verify

The safest first tasks share three traits. The input arrives in a predictable form. The output can be judged against a source document or a short rule list. An error can be caught before it reaches a customer or a public page.

| Task | Why it fits an early handover | Human check that should stay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sorting incoming inquiries by topic and urgency | Messages fall into a small set of categories that the office can define in advance | Review the urgent and unclassified folders each day |
| Drafting replies to routine questions | Answers come from approved material such as hours, service scope, and process steps | Approve any reply that mentions dates, documents, or individual cases |
| Summarizing long documents and meeting notes | The source text is present, so summary claims can be traced back | Check the summary against the source before circulating it |
| Assembling a weekly report from existing files | Figures and status lines already exist in spreadsheets or calendars | Confirm sources are current before the report goes out |
| Answering frequent website questions from an approved knowledge base | Questions repeat, and approved answers can be reused with little variation | Review unanswered questions weekly and update the knowledge base |

Website questions deserve a note. A public site built as static pages can still carry a small assistant for routine questions, with dynamic services attached only where needed. We covered that layout in [Static-first: why most small-business websites don't need a server](2026-11-16-static-first-websites.md). The assistant should use approved material and hand off to a person when a visitor asks about an individual order, a quotation, or a complaint.

## A handover test you can run in one meeting

1. Can we write the steps on one page, including what to do when information is missing?
2. Is there a source of truth the system must use, such as a price list, a policy page, or a case file?
3. Can a reviewer check the output in less time than doing the task from scratch?
4. Does an error stay internal, or does it reach a customer within minutes?
5. Who owns the task when the system flags something it cannot resolve?

Clear answers to questions 1 to 3, with errors staying internal, make a task a reasonable candidate. If output leaves the office immediately and cannot be recalled, keep a human approval step for the first months.

## Tasks that should stay with people

**Money movement.** Approving payments, issuing refunds, changing bank details, and confirming payroll figures belong to an accountable person. Software can gather invoices, match them to purchase records, and prepare a payment list. The release step stays human.

**Formal commitments.** Quotations, contract terms, promised delivery dates, and responses to legal or regulatory letters shape the position of the business. An assistant can prepare a draft. A person with authority signs off and sends.

**Sensitive personnel matters.** Hiring decisions, performance warnings, medical or family circumstances, and internal disputes involve judgment and confidentiality. Scheduling and filing around these matters can be assisted. The decisions and the conversations stay human.

**Customers who are already upset.** A complaint involving loss or delay needs a person who can listen and make amends within clear limits. Software can summarize the case history so the person starts informed.

**Anything irreversible without a log.** Actions that delete records, publish publicly, or change access rights need a named approver and a record of what changed. Where no reliable log exists, set up logging before delegation.

## Guardrails that make delegation workable

Write the job down. A one-page procedure listing inputs, sources, output format, and escalation rules prevents many early failures. When the procedure changes, update the page and note the date.

Run draft mode first. For the first two weeks, the system prepares work and a person releases it. Sample daily at the start, then reduce sampling as the error pattern becomes clear.

Limit access by task. An assistant that summarizes meetings does not need payroll files. Give each role the smallest set of folders and systems its procedure requires, and review permissions when duties change.

Define the handoff sentence. Every role needs a clear stop rule, for example: "If the request mentions a refund, a legal deadline, or a named complaint, stop and route it to the office manager with the source material attached." A written stop rule can be tested.

Keep a simple log. Date, task, source used, output produced, and reviewer decision are enough. The log shows which tasks run cleanly and which still consume review time.

## A practical two-week rollout

Days 1 to 3: choose one task from the table and write its procedure. Days 4 to 7: run draft mode and check every output. Days 8 to 10: fix the procedure where drafts went wrong, and add stop rules for new cases. Days 11 to 14: sample part of the output, provided earlier checks were clean. Then decide from the log whether to keep, adjust, or withdraw the handover. Delegation only helps when review takes less effort than the original task and errors surface early.

# 中文版：小办公室的 AI 数字员工——哪些任务先交出去，哪些永远不要交

> **摘要**——小办公室用 AI 数字员工，首批任务选得对，后面才省心：重复性强、规则清楚、产出容易核对的活儿最适合先交，比如分拣咨询、起草常规回复、整理摘要、汇总周报。涉及动钱、正式承诺、敏感人事和客户投诉的事项，应当留在具体的人手里。做法上先走草稿模式，人抽查确认稳定后，再逐步扩大范围。

所谓 AI 数字员工，是在人的监督下承担一项明确办公室工作的软件。它读取进来的材料，按写好的流程办事，交出一份草稿或完成一个常规步骤，并把做过什么记录下来。这个定义要先说清。一句含糊的「把邮箱管起来」，给系统的空间太大，给负责人留下的核对抓手又太少。

起步顺利的办公室，通常都从窄处开始：挑一件输入明确、产出明确、出错能被很快看出来的活儿。

## 先交容易核对的工作

适合首批交出的任务有几个共同点。输入形式相对固定；产出对错可以拿源文件或一份短规则来判；错误能在抵达客户、银行或公开页面之前被拦下。

| 任务 | 为什么适合先交 | 应保留的人工核对 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 按主题和紧急程度分拣进来的咨询 | 类别就那么几类，办公室可以事先定清 | 每天看一遍紧急和未分类两组 |
| 起草常规问题的回复 | 答案来自已定稿的材料，如营业时间、服务范围、办理步骤 | 凡涉及日期、证件材料、个案情况的回复，先经人批准 |
| 汇总长文档和会议记录 | 源文本就在手边，摘要里的说法可以回溯核对 | 传阅前拿摘要对一遍原文 |
| 用现有文件拼周报 | 数字和进度本来就在表格、工单或日历里 | 发出前确认源文件是最新版 |
| 用已审定的知识库回答网站常见问题 | 问题高度重复，定稿答案可以反复用 | 每周看一遍没答上的问题，及时更新知识库 |

网站答疑多说一句。公开网站做成静态页面，照样可以挂一个小助手答常规问题，动态服务只接在真正需要的地方。这个布局我们在[《静态优先：大多数小企业网站，其实不需要一台服务器》](2026-11-16-static-first-websites.md)里写过。助手答题只能用企业审定过的材料；访客问到个人订单、报价或投诉，就转给真人。

## 一次会议就能跑完的交接测试

交任务之前，把现在做这件活儿的人叫来，逐条过这份清单。

1. 步骤能不能写在一页纸上，包括信息缺失时怎么办？
2. 有没有系统必须依据的准绳，比如价目表、政策页或个案档案？
3. 核对产出的时间，是不是比从头做一遍更短？
4. 出了错是留在内部，还是几分钟内就到客户手里？
5. 系统碰到处理不了的情况，这件活儿归谁接？

前三条都有明确答案、第四条指向内部错误，这个任务就值得试。若产出一出门就收不回来，至少在头几个月里保留人工批准这一步。

## 应当留在人手里的任务

有些工作的后果，不是事后看一眼草稿就能挽回的。

**动钱的事。** 批准付款、退款、变更收款账户、确认工资数字，都要落在能担责的人身上。软件可以收集发票、和采购记录对账、把付款清单备好；放行这一步，人来做。

**正式承诺。** 报价、合同条款、向某个具体客户许下的交付日期、回给法律或监管方面的函件，都关系到企业的立场。助手可以备背景材料、起草稿；有权拍板的人审定后再发。

**敏感人事。** 录用决定、绩效警告、医疗或家庭情况、内部纠纷，既要判断，也涉隐私。这类沟通哪怕措辞看着客气，交给自动化发出去也可能伤人。这类事项周边的排期和归档可以让软件帮手，决定和谈话本身留给人。

**已经带着情绪的客户。** 投诉里有损失、有拖延，或者客户觉得自己被晾着，这时需要一个能听、能在明确权限内补救的人。软件可以把个案的来龙去脉先整理好，让接手的人心里有数；对话本身不要让它独立进行。

**没有日志就不可逆的动作。** 删记录、往公开渠道发内容、改访问权限，这类动作要有具名批准人，也要留变更记录。还没有可靠日志的地方，先把日志建起来，再谈交接。

## 让交接站得住的几道护栏

把活儿写下来。一页纸的流程，列清输入、依据、产出格式和上报规则，头一批失败多半能挡住。流程有变，纸面跟着改，并标上日期。

先跑草稿模式。新任务的头两周，系统只备活儿，人来放行。开头几天份份看，等错误形态摸清了，再降低抽查比例。

权限按任务给。负责汇总会议的助手，用不着碰工资文件。每个角色只给它流程里用得到的文件夹、邮箱和系统；人员或职责有变动时，权限跟着复核。

把转交的话写成明确规则。每个角色都要有一条停下来的规矩，比如：「请求里出现退款、法律期限或具名投诉，停下，把源材料附上，转给办公室负责人。」含糊的自信没法审计，写明的停机规则可以拿来测试。

日志从简。对小办公室来说，日期、任务、用了什么依据、产出了什么、审核人怎么定，这几项就够了。日志能看出哪些任务跑得顺，哪些还在吃审核时间。

## 两周落地的走法

第 1 到 3 天：从上表挑一件任务，把一页纸流程写出来。第 4 到 7 天：跑草稿模式，产出份份核对。第 8 到 10 天：草稿出错的地方改流程，实际碰到的情况补进停机规则。第 11 到 14 天：前期核对干净的话，改抽查一部分。两周结束，拿着日志决定：这件交接保留、调整，还是撤回。

有些老板觉得这个节奏慢。它护住的恰恰是交接的本意：审核比亲手做更省力，错误能早冒头，交出去才划算。
