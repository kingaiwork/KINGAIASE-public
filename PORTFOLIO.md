## Public-information principle

Public repositories and public pages should answer five questions clearly:

1. What is the product?
2. Who is it for?
3. What problem does it solve?
4. How can it create business value?
5. How can a customer, partner, journalist or investor contact the team?

They should not disclose proprietary implementation details simply to appear technical.
