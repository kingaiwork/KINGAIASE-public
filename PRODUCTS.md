## Five customer experience domains

The main KING AI website organizes the ecosystem around five customer-facing domains. These are experience and solution directions; individual product status must still be checked before production adoption.

### Personal — KINGAI Life

Briefings, tasks, documents, reminders and longer-term context brought into one place so intelligence can continue from what matters instead of starting over each time. Sensitive actions remain bounded by permissions and approval.

### Family — KINGAI Family

Shared calendars, important records, reminders and bounded family memory, designed to improve coordination without treating family sharing as permission to expose everything to everyone.

### Business — KINGAI Workforce

Governed digital workers with defined roles, tasks and authority for repetitive service, sales, operations and other workflows where approval and auditability matter.

### Organizations & Nations — KINGAI Sovereign

Institutional and sovereign deployment direction with explicit identity, data, model and execution boundaries for organizations that require stronger control and accountability. Exact production scope is project-specific.

### Global Intelligence — KINGAI World Intelligence

Decision-intelligence direction for organizing public world signals into source-aware briefings, timelines, watchlists and risk context while keeping evidence visible.

Across these domains, KING AI treats model capability and execution authority as separate concerns. Identity, permissions, approvals and audit evidence should remain explicit, especially for higher-risk work.

## 五大客户体验领域

KING AI 主官网按五类真实用户场景组织产品。这里描述的是产品体验与解决方案方向；用于正式生产前，仍应以各产品实际状态和项目范围为准。

### 个人 — KINGAI Life

把简报、任务、文档、提醒和长期上下文放在一起，让系统不用每次从零开始。涉及敏感操作时，仍受权限和确认机制约束。

### 家庭 — KINGAI Family

共享家庭日历、重要资料、提醒和有边界的家庭记忆，让一家人更好协作，同时不把“家庭共享”变成“所有内容对所有人开放”。

### 企业 — KINGAI Workforce

面向企业的受治理数字员工方向，可以为 AI 分配岗位、任务和权限，适合客服、销售、运营等重复工作，并保留审批与审计边界。

### 组织 / 国家 — KINGAI Sovereign

面向机构与主权部署的方向，把身份、数据、模型和执行边界明确分开，适合需要更强控制与问责的大型组织。具体生产范围按项目确认。

### 全球情报 — KINGAI World Intelligence

面向决策的全球情报方向，把公开世界信号整理成带来源的简报、时间线、关注列表和风险背景，减少信息过载，同时保留证据线索。

贯穿五大领域的共同原则是：模型能力与执行权限分离。身份、权限、审批和审计证据需要保持明确，风险越高越需要清晰的人类控制和恢复路径。
