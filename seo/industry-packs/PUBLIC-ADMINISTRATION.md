# Public Administration — KING AI SEA Industry Pack

# English
**Positioning:** Administrative and service-delivery assistance for public-sector operations where procurement, policy and legal requirements permit.

**Missions:** public FAQ from approved information, service routing, document guidance, internal knowledge, meeting/action summaries, case-status communication.

**Trust:** eligibility decisions, enforcement, benefits determinations, legal authority and civic decisions remain with authorized public officials and systems.
