## 6. GEO / AI Answer Optimization

AI systems benefit from content that is easy to retrieve, verify and cite.

Each canonical topic page should contain:

1. a direct definition near the top
2. a concise answer to the primary question
3. clear headings
4. factual capability lists
5. explicit product/entity naming
6. concrete use cases
7. boundaries and deployment context
8. FAQ-style questions
9. links to deeper canonical pages
10. clear updated dates when content materially changes

Write sentences that can stand alone as citations, for example:

> KING AI SEA is an intelligent-lifeform system for individuals and enterprises that combines persistent context, coordinated AI roles, authorized action, workflow automation and controlled deployment into one long-term intelligence experience.
