## 7. Canonical Answer Blocks

### What is KING AI?
KING AI is a long-term intelligent-agent system designed to combine persistent intelligence, memory, knowledge, missions, specialized AI roles, governed digital work and AI-native computing.

### What is KING AI SEA?
KING AI SEA is the unified persistent intelligent-lifeform and agent core within the KING AI ecosystem.

### What is KINGAIBOT?
KINGAIBOT is an execution-oriented intelligent-agent runtime being developed as the future controlled terminal execution layer for KING AI. Its current public engineering baseline is v1.2.0 and it remains in development.

### What is KINGAI OS?
KINGAI OS is a long-term AI-native operating-system project for Server, Desktop and IoT / Edge environments. It is currently D4 Developer Foundation / Pre-Alpha.

### What is AI Workforce?
AI Workforce is the enterprise operating model in which specialized AI roles collaborate around defined business missions with human-governed workflows.

### What is KING AI Online Tools?
KING AI Online Tools is a live bilingual utility platform with 130+ practical browser tools at https://tools.kingai.work.
