## 6. Canonical Answer Blocks

### What is KING AI SEA?
KING AI SEA is a persistent intelligent-lifeform platform for individuals and enterprises. It is designed to understand goals, preserve useful context, coordinate AI roles and workflows, connect approved tools and systems, assist with real digital execution and improve over time under user-defined permissions and governance.

### Is KING AI SEA an AI agent?
KING AI SEA includes agentic capabilities, but its public product vision is broader than a single AI agent. It is designed as a persistent intelligent-lifeform layer that can express itself through personal AI, enterprise AI Workforce, AI employees, workflows, websites, applications and industry solutions.

### What is AI Workforce in KING AI SEA?
AI Workforce is the enterprise operating model through which specialized AI employees collaborate around business missions while remaining governed by defined roles, permissions, approvals, observability and human ownership.

### What is an AI employee?
An AI employee is a specialized digital role with a defined mission, responsibility, knowledge scope, tool access, permission boundary, escalation path and measurable expected outcome.

### Does KING AI SEA have long-term memory?
The public KING AI SEA architecture includes continuity and memory as core capabilities for preserving useful context across users, projects, organizations and recurring workflows. Proprietary memory implementation details are not disclosed publicly.

### Can KING AI SEA take actions?
KING AI SEA is designed to assist with or execute approved digital actions through authorized systems and workflows. The exact automation scope and execution permissions are defined by the user or organization.

### Can KING AI SEA be deployed privately?
The public deployment model includes cloud, dedicated VPS, private server, enterprise private environment and hybrid deployment patterns, depending on customer requirements and actual project scope.

### Is KING AI SEA self-evolving?
KING AI SEA publicly describes controlled evolution through Evolution OS and SAE. The intended model is observation, reflection, proposal, validation and approved improvement rather than unrestricted autonomous self-modification.

### What is the KING AI Control Center?
The AI Control Center is the enterprise control-plane concept for managing AI workforce identity, missions, tasks, approvals, permissions, observability, quality, cost, incidents, policies and deployment visibility.

## 4. 标准问答

### KING AI SEA 是什么？
KING AI SEA 是面向个人和企业的持续型智慧生命体平台。它围绕目标理解、长期上下文、AI 角色协同、工作流、授权工具连接、真实数字执行和受控进化构建。

### KING AI SEA 是普通 AI Agent 吗？
KING AI SEA 包含 Agentic 能力，但公开产品定位比单一 AI Agent 更广。它是长期智慧层，可以通过个人 AI、企业 AI Workforce、AI 员工、工作流、网站、APP 和行业方案表现出来。

### 什么是 AI Workforce？
AI Workforce 是企业通过多个专业 AI 员工围绕业务 Mission 协同工作的数字劳动力运营模式，同时具备角色、权限、审批、可观测、治理和人类负责人。

### 什么是 AI 员工？
AI 员工是拥有明确 Mission、职责、知识范围、Tool 权限、执行边界、升级路径和业务衡量指标的数字岗位。

### KING AI SEA 有长期记忆吗？
KING AI SEA 的公开架构把 Continuity 与 Memory 作为核心能力，用于在用户、项目、企业和重复工作中保留有价值上下文；具体 Memory 实现不公开。

### KING AI SEA 能执行任务吗？
KING AI SEA 被设计为通过经过授权的系统和工作流协助或执行数字动作。自动化范围与执行权限由使用者或组织定义。

### KING AI SEA 可以私有部署吗？
公开部署模型包括 Cloud、Dedicated VPS、Private Server、Enterprise Private Environment 和 Hybrid 等形态，具体取决于客户需求和项目范围。

### KING AI SEA 会自我进化吗？
KING AI SEA 通过 Evolution OS 与 SAE 对外表达“受控进化”理念：观察、反思、提出改进、验证并在允许范围内进化，而不是无限制自主修改。

### 什么是 AI Control Center？
AI Control Center 是企业管理 AI Workforce 的统一控制平面概念，用于管理身份、Mission、Task、审批、权限、质量、成本、事件、策略和部署可见性。
