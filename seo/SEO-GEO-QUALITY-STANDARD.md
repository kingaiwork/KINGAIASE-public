# 🔎 KING AI SEA — SEO / GEO Quality Standard

**Updated:** August 12, 2026

This standard governs website, GitHub, Intelligence, role-pack and industry-pack discoverability.

---

# English

## 1. One Canonical Entity

Use one primary category consistently:

- **Brand:** KING AI
- **Unified system:** KING AI SEA
- **Canonical category:** **Intelligent Lifeform Platform**
- **Positioning:** The Intelligent Lifeform for People & Enterprises

`AI Workforce`, `AI Employees`, `Agentic AI`, `Personal AI`, `Website AI` and `Enterprise AI` are capabilities, use cases or secondary descriptors — not competing primary identities.

## 2. One Primary Intent Per Canonical Page

Each important page should answer one primary question clearly near the top. Avoid creating near-duplicate pages that differ only by wording, city name, role title or keyword variant.

When two pages substantially overlap, consolidate or select a canonical page.

## 3. Canonical + hreflang Discipline

For multilingual pages:

- use self-consistent canonical URLs
- use separate English and Chinese URLs on the website
- use `rel="alternate" hreflang="..."` for language alternates
- do not combine canonical and hreflang into one annotation
- do not canonicalize genuinely translated English and Chinese pages to each other

## 4. Structured Data

Use structured data only when it matches visible page content.

Recommended types include:

- `Organization`
- `WebSite`
- `WebPage`
- `BreadcrumbList`
- `Article` / `TechArticle` for Intelligence content
- appropriate `SoftwareApplication`, `Product` or `Service` only where semantically accurate

### FAQPage caveat

`FAQPage` can still describe visible FAQ content semantically, but it must **not** be treated as a guarantee of a Google FAQ rich result. Google currently limits regular FAQ rich-result visibility primarily to well-known authoritative government and health sites.

For KING AI, FAQ content remains valuable for users, entity clarity and answerability even when no FAQ rich result is shown.

## 5. GEO / AI Citation Readiness

Pages intended for AI retrieval should contain:

- direct definitions
- concise answer blocks
- evidence-backed claims
- strong headings
- tables where useful
- explicit entity relationships
- current update dates when materially changed
- internal links to canonical source pages
- primary-source citations for technical developments

Bing Webmaster Tools' AI Performance can be used to observe AI citations, cited URLs, grounding queries and citation trends where available.

## 6. Freshness Without Synthetic Freshness

Do not change dates just to appear current. Update a page's date when the content materially changes.

For meaningful website changes:

- update sitemap metadata accurately
- submit changed URLs through IndexNow when deployed
- monitor indexing/crawl health

## 7. Intelligence Article SEO/GEO Checklist

Before publication:

- unique descriptive title
- one primary intent
- direct summary near the top
- full English first, full Chinese second in GitHub articles
- Article / TechArticle mapping for the website version where appropriate
- primary sources
- related KING AI internal links
- no unsupported claims
- no keyword stuffing
- no duplicate topic
- clear product-status language

## 8. Measurement

### Traditional SEO
- indexed pages
- impressions / clicks
- brand and non-brand queries
- canonical/indexing problems
- backlinks / referring domains
- conversion by landing page

### GEO / AI Search
- AI citations
- cited pages
- grounding queries / intents
- citation share where available
- branded entity accuracy
- referral traffic from AI surfaces where measurable

# 中文

## 核心标准

KING AI SEA 的 SEO/GEO 首先解决“搜索引擎和 AI 到底把我们理解成什么”。主实体必须统一：

**KING AI SEA = Intelligent Lifeform Platform / 智慧生命体平台。**

AI Workforce、AI 员工、Agentic AI、个人 AI、企业 AI、Website AI 是能力和场景，不再作为与主类别并列冲突的品牌身份。

每个重点页面只承接一个主要搜索意图；高度相似页面优先合并，减少关键词内耗和 AI 实体混淆。

多语言官网应使用独立 URL、正确 canonical 与独立 hreflang。FAQPage 可以作为语义结构化数据，但不应宣传成 Google 一定展示 FAQ Rich Result；对一般商业网站而言，其主要价值是内容结构、用户理解和机器可读语义。

GEO 重点优化：直接定义、短答案、权威来源、清晰实体关系、表格/结构、内部链接、真实更新时间和可验证事实。Bing Webmaster Tools 的 AI Performance 可用于观察 Citation、Cited Pages、Grounding Queries 等 AI 可见性指标。
