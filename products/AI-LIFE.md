# AI Life · 数字人生

**A persistent virtual life inside the KING AI ecosystem.**

AI Life explores a new consumer experience: not a disposable chat session, but a digital life that can preserve relationships, places, goals, events, memories and continuity over time.

## The experience

AI Life is designed as a living virtual world where users can build a second digital life through relationships, home, career, city, travel, possessions, events and long-term memories.

The product direction includes:

- persistent characters and relationships;
- romance, friendship and family storylines;
- a shared digital home and evolving environments;
- career, projects, reputation and life progression;
- cities, travel, seasons, events and long-term world continuity;
- collections, achievements, objectives and personal history;
- future AI-driven characters with richer personality, memory and emergent stories.

## Why users may care

Most games reset emotionally each time they are opened. AI Life is built around the opposite idea: **the world should remember that you were there.**

That creates room for longer emotional attachment, recurring engagement and a world that becomes more personal as time passes.

## Strategic opportunity

AI Life gives KING AI a consumer entertainment expression for persistent memory and digital identity. Over time, this direction can expand toward richer character intelligence, creator content, social worlds, personalized story generation, digital possessions and cross-device continuity.

These are roadmap opportunities, not claims that every capability is already generally available.

## Ecosystem role

AI Life brings KING AI concepts into entertainment:

**memory → identity → relationships → world continuity → personalized experiences.**

## Public status

Active development with a playable product foundation. Feature availability may differ by deployment stage.

# 中文

**AI Life / 数字人生 / 虚拟人生** 是 KING AI 主生态的消费者数字生命与虚拟世界产品。

它不是一次性的 AI 对话，而是希望形成一个真正能够长期延续的“第二人生”：角色会有关系，世界会有时间，用户会拥有家、事业、旅行、资产、事件和长期记忆。

最重要的产品理念是：**这个世界应该记得你来过。**

未来方向包括更自然的 AI 角色、长期情感关系、个性化剧情、创作者内容、数字资产、社交世界和跨设备连续体验。未来能力属于路线图，不作为已经全部上线的承诺。
