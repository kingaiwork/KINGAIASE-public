# KING AI Public Repository Consolidation

**Current model: one active public hub + official product websites + legacy public redirect surfaces.**

Main system: https://www.kingai.work/  
Business: vip@kingai.work

## English

### Why KING AI consolidated the public layer

Maintaining a separate active public repository for every product creates avoidable problems: duplicated descriptions, split SEO/GEO authority, stale product claims, inconsistent release links, repeated customer knowledge and a larger surface for accidental technical disclosure.

KING AI therefore uses this repository as the **single active public commercial, product-discovery, customer-safe knowledge and verified-release hub** for the main ecosystem.

Official product websites remain the preferred place to experience each product. Private production authorities remain the only source and implementation authorities.

### What belongs here

This public hub may contain:

- product positioning and user-facing explanations;
- ecosystem navigation;
- customer-safe FAQs and approved public knowledge;
- commercial and partnership information;
- public roadmap language clearly labeled as future direction;
- verified public release references;
- SEO/GEO and AI-readable public facts.

### What never belongs here

This hub must not publish or mirror:

- production source code;
- internal architecture or infrastructure topology;
- private APIs or database design;
- credentials, secrets or signing material;
- proprietary algorithms or model orchestration;
- security implementation details;
- privileged operations or deployment internals;
- confidential customer or operational information.

### Active product discovery

The unified hub covers the public discovery layer for:

- KING AI / SEA
- KINGAIBOT
- KINGAI OS
- KINGAI OPS
- KINGAI Security
- KINGAI Office
- KING AI Customer OS
- KING AI Online Tools
- KINGAI Pet
- AI Life
- Digital Life

USDX TECH is an independent related business surface and may be referenced for business discovery, but it is not counted as a core KING AI product.

Founder personal projects remain separate and are not absorbed into the KING AI portfolio.

### Legacy public repositories

Older product-specific public repositories may remain visible for historical links, existing search references and migration continuity. Their current default branches should clearly state that they are **legacy discovery/history surfaces**.

They must:

1. point users to this unified hub and/or the relevant official product website;
2. avoid creating a second competing product homepage;
3. avoid new implementation or deployment documentation;
4. avoid acting as current source, installation or release authority;
5. preserve truthful historical context without presenting old material as current.

Where repository administration becomes available, legacy repositories can be archived after migration, historical release and link requirements are checked.

### Public release authority

Current downloadable release truth converges here. Product-specific historical releases can remain where necessary, but a legacy repository does not become current production or release authority merely because an old asset exists there.

See `RELEASES.md`.

### Why this helps users

A user should be able to answer three questions quickly:

1. What is the product?
2. Where is the official current experience?
3. Where is the trusted public information or release reference?

The unified model provides one consistent answer instead of many competing repositories.

# 中文

## 为什么公开层要统一

过去如果每个产品都维护一个独立活跃公开仓，会逐渐产生：介绍重复、SEO/GEO 权威分散、产品信息不同步、发布链接冲突、客服知识重复，以及公开技术信息越来越难管理的问题。

因此 KING AI 现在采用：

**一个统一公开中心 + 各产品官方网站 + 旧公开仓迁移入口。**

本仓是 KING AI 主生态唯一活跃的公开商业、产品发现、客户安全知识和已验证发布信息中心。

### 本仓可以公开什么

产品定位、用户场景、生态导航、FAQ、批准的客户知识、商业与合作信息、明确标注的未来方向、公开发布引用，以及 SEO/GEO/AI 可读事实。

### 本仓不能公开什么

生产源码、内部架构、基础设施拓扑、私有 API、数据库结构、密钥、签名材料、专有算法、模型编排、安全实现、部署细节、特权操作或客户机密。

### 旧公开仓怎么处理

旧产品公开仓只保留搜索、旧链接和历史价值。当前默认分支应该明确告诉用户：

**这是 legacy 入口，不再是当前产品主页、源码权威、安装权威或发布权威。**

旧仓应把用户带到本统一公开中心或对应产品官方网站。未来具备仓库管理权限后，在确认历史发布和链接迁移安全的前提下再归档。

这样可以让用户、搜索引擎、合作伙伴和 AI 系统都获得更一致、更可信的 KING AI 信息。
