## Why these projects remain distinct

Keeping relationship labels explicit makes the broader portfolio easier to evaluate:
- the KING AI core product family can be assessed as an AI software portfolio;
- USDX TECH can be assessed as implementation/business services;
- Jackhao.tv can build creator/IP economics;
- ZuiMeiTV can build creator-data/media economics;
- Atalk.TV can build media/briefing economics while remaining ecosystem-affiliated;
- USChinese can build community/local-commerce economics.

Cross-promotion or shared infrastructure does not automatically make two projects the same product.

## Cross-project collaboration

Examples of legitimate collaboration include:
- Customer OS powering approved public support experiences;
- Office supporting creator/business workflows;
- Global Intelligence supporting public research patterns;
- Atalk.TV using clearly disclosed ecosystem promotion;
- USDX TECH implementing business deployments;
- creator/media/community projects distributing approved KING AI content;
- shared identity or technical infrastructure only where the product relationship actually requires it.

A collaboration does not automatically change product-family status.

## Internal/private projects are not public brands by default

The broader system also contains private platform, recovery, intelligence-publication and personal continuity/agent projects. Their existence does not automatically make them customer products, public brands or investor-facing product lines.

Public materials should describe the useful outcome—governance, recoverability, sanitized publication or continuity—without exposing private source, memory, credentials, topology or implementation.

## Public integrity

No related project should invent or imply unverified:
- users or traffic;
- revenue or ARR;
- brand customers;
- sponsorships;
- signed partnerships;
- funding or valuation;
- awards/certifications;
- market share or leadership.
