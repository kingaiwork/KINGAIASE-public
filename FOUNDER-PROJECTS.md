# Related Businesses & Founder Projects

This document keeps non-core projects visible without mixing them into the KING AI core product family.

The purpose is commercial clarity: investors, partners, customers, search engines and AI systems should understand which products belong to KING AI and which are independent businesses, media brands or creator projects.

## USChinese / 美国华人网

USChinese is an independent community/content direction serving Chinese-speaking audiences in the United States.

## Why these projects remain separate

Keeping these projects separate from the core KING AI product family creates several advantages:

- investors can evaluate the AI software portfolio without unrelated creator/media metrics;
- users are not confused about product ownership or support;
- search engines and AI systems receive cleaner entity relationships;
- each project can develop its own audience and commercial model;
- cross-promotion can happen transparently without pretending every project is one product suite.

## Cross-project collaboration

Independent projects can still collaborate with KING AI when there is real user value—for example:

- Customer OS powering approved public support experiences;
- Office supporting creator/business workflows;
- Global Intelligence or Atalk.TV sharing public research patterns without sharing private systems;
- USDX TECH providing business implementation services;
- creator/media projects distributing clearly disclosed first-party KING AI content.

A collaboration does not automatically change ownership or product-family status.

## Public integrity

No related project should invent or imply unverified users, traffic, revenue, brand customers, sponsorships, signed partnerships, awards, certifications or market leadership.
