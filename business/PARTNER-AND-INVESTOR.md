# KING AI Partner & Investor Overview

## English

KING AI is a long-term intelligent-agent system project focused on persistent intelligence, governed execution, AI Workforce, interoperability and AI-native computing.

This document is for enterprises, technology partners, research organizations, distributors and strategic investors evaluating the public direction of the project.

## Strategic thesis

Most AI products begin and end with a model interaction. KING AI is being developed around a broader system:

```text
Persistent Memory
      +
Verified Intelligence
      +
Mission Planning
      +
Specialized AI Roles
      +
Governed Execution
      +
AI-native Computing
```

The long-term objective is to create intelligent systems that can remain useful over time, connect to approved digital systems and help move work from information to outcomes while keeping authority bounded and reviewable.
