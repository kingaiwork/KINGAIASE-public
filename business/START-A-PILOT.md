# Start a KING AI Pilot

## English

You do not need to transform the whole company to learn whether persistent intelligence is useful.

Start with **one recurring workflow that already costs time, attention or consistency**.

> **The best first AI project is usually not the biggest one. It is the one where improvement can be seen clearly.**

✉️ **vip@kingai.work**

## Pick one starting point

### Knowledge Pilot

Choose this if your team has valuable information but people struggle to find, reuse or preserve it.

Good candidates:

- internal policies
- SOPs
- product knowledge
- technical documentation
- research archives
- customer knowledge
- onboarding material

A focused pilot can evaluate whether KING AI can make approved knowledge easier to access and more useful in recurring work.

### Workflow Pilot

Choose this if a process repeats every day or every week.

Good candidates:

- recurring research
- document preparation
- operations follow-up
- reporting
- information triage
- task coordination
- recurring monitoring

The objective is to remove avoidable repetition while preserving human review where it matters.

### Customer Experience Pilot

Choose this if customers repeatedly need help understanding what to buy, what to do next or where to find an answer.

Good candidates:

- product discovery
- service explanation
- customer onboarding
- knowledge support
- guided self-service
- lead qualification support

The objective is not simply to add another chatbot. It is to help more users reach a useful next step.

### Intelligence Pilot

Choose this if your team repeatedly tracks the same market, technology, competitor or public-information categories.

Good candidates:

- industry monitoring
- technology radar
- competitor observation
- public-source research
- management briefs
- recurring evidence collection

The objective is to turn repeated information gathering into a more consistent intelligence workflow.

## What to bring

A useful first conversation only needs five things:

1. **The workflow** — what keeps repeating?
2. **The people** — who owns the result?
3. **The information** — where do the inputs come from?
4. **The friction** — what is slow, repetitive, inconsistent or easily missed?
5. **The desired improvement** — what would “better” look like?

You do not need a finished AI strategy.

## What a good pilot should answer

A pilot should help determine:

- Is the workflow suitable for intelligent assistance?
- Can the required information be made usable?
- Where should humans remain in the loop?
- Which steps can be simplified?
- What should be measured?
- Is the value strong enough to justify expansion?

## Keep the first scope small enough to learn

A good pilot usually focuses on:

- one workflow
- one owner or team
- one defined information set
- one clear outcome
- a small number of success measures

If it works, expand from evidence rather than enthusiasm.

## What happens after a successful pilot

Depending on the result, the next step may be:

- improve the same workflow
- add more knowledge
- connect another approved system
- add a specialized AI role
- introduce a second workflow
- create a broader AI Workforce design
- evaluate private or hybrid deployment
- build a longer-term managed service relationship

There is no requirement to expand unless the result justifies it.

# 中文

## 从一个真正的问题开始

你不需要先把整个公司“AI 化”，才能判断 KING AI 是否有价值。

先找一件**每天、每周都在重复，而且正在消耗时间、注意力或一致性的工作**。

> **最好的第一个 AI 项目，往往不是最大的项目，而是改善前后最容易看清楚的那个。**

✉️ **vip@kingai.work**

## 选择一个起点

### Knowledge Pilot · 知识 Pilot

适合：企业资料很多，但员工需要时经常找不到、重复问、重复整理。

可以从制度、SOP、产品资料、技术文档、研究资料、客服知识或培训资料开始。

### Workflow Pilot · 流程 Pilot

适合：每天或每周都重复发生的工作。

例如研究、文档准备、运营跟进、报告、资料分类、任务协调和持续监控。

### Customer Experience Pilot · 客户体验 Pilot

适合：客户经常不知道买什么、下一步做什么、答案在哪里。

可以围绕产品发现、服务说明、客户引导、知识支持、自助服务或线索支持开始。

目标不是“加一个聊天框”，而是让更多用户真正走到有价值的下一步。

### Intelligence Pilot · 情报 Pilot

适合：团队长期重复关注相同的行业、技术、竞争或公开信息。

可以从行业监控、技术雷达、竞争观察、公开研究和管理层简报开始。

## 你只需要准备五件事

1. **流程**：什么事情一直在重复？
2. **负责人**：谁对结果负责？
3. **资料**：输入主要来自哪里？
4. **痛点**：哪里最慢、最重复、最不稳定、最容易漏？
5. **目标**：什么样的结果算明显变好了？

不需要先准备一整套 AI 战略。

## 一个 Pilot 应该回答什么

- 这个流程适不适合 AI 辅助？
- 资料能不能被有效组织和使用？
- 哪里必须保留人工判断？
- 哪些步骤可以明显简化？
- 应该衡量什么？
- 结果值不值得继续扩大？

## 第一阶段故意做小

一个好的第一阶段通常只需要：

- 一个流程
- 一个负责人或团队
- 一组明确资料
- 一个清晰结果
- 少量可衡量指标

如果有效，再根据证据扩大，而不是因为“AI 很热”就扩大。

## 成功之后可以怎么走

根据实际结果，可以继续：

- 优化同一个流程
- 增加更多知识
- 连接另一个经过批准的系统
- 增加一个专业 AI 岗位
- 扩展第二个工作流
- 形成更完整的 AI Workforce
- 评估私有或混合部署
- 建立长期托管与持续优化合作

是否扩大，由实际价值决定。
