# One Useful AI Role Is Better Than Ten Generic Agents

**Published:** August 13, 2026  
**Topics:** AI Workforce · AI Employees · Enterprise AI · Business Operations

## The idea

When a company starts exploring AI, it is tempting to create many agents at once: sales, marketing, research, support, operations and more.

But a smaller question is usually more useful:

> **What is one responsibility an AI role could own well enough that the business would notice?**

A clear role is easier to evaluate, easier to improve and easier to connect to a real business outcome.

## A title is not a job

“Marketing Agent” sounds useful, but marketing includes research, planning, writing, campaign operations, reporting and customer insight.

A better first role might be:

- weekly competitor research;
- product knowledge assistant;
- customer FAQ preparation;
- campaign performance summary;
- lead-research preparation;
- recurring operations report.

The narrower the responsibility, the easier it is to define what good work looks like.

## What makes an AI role useful?

A useful role should have:

1. **a clear responsibility** — what result is it helping produce?
2. **known inputs** — what information does it use?
3. **a repeatable workflow** — what happens each time?
4. **a clear output** — what does it produce?
5. **a measurable benefit** — what becomes faster, easier or more consistent?

That is enough to create a meaningful first pilot.

## Start with boring work

The best first AI role is often not the most futuristic one.

It may be the role that prepares the same report every Monday, organizes incoming research, summarizes a fixed set of information, helps staff find internal knowledge or prepares documents before a person reviews them.

This kind of work is attractive because the value is visible.

If a task used to take three hours and now takes one hour with a better first draft, the improvement is understandable.

## Then build an AI Workforce

Once one role works well, the organization can add other specialized roles around the same mission.

```text
One recurring problem
      ↓
One useful AI role
      ↓
One measurable result
      ↓
A repeatable workflow
      ↓
More specialized roles
      ↓
AI Workforce
```

This is more practical than starting with a large collection of generic agents and hoping useful workflows appear later.

## A simple buying test

Before paying for another AI product, ask:

- Which recurring responsibility are we trying to improve?
- Who owns the outcome?
- What information is needed?
- What should the AI produce?
- How will we know whether it helped?

If those five answers are clear, the project is ready for a much better conversation.

# 中文

## 一个真正有用的 AI 岗位，比十个泛化 Agent 更有价值

企业刚开始尝试 AI 时，很容易一次创建很多 Agent：销售、营销、研究、客服、运营……

但更有效的问题其实是：

> **有没有一个明确责任，可以交给一个 AI 岗位去持续做好，而且业务人员能够明显感受到价值？**

“Marketing Agent”只是一个名字。真正的营销工作可能包含市场研究、内容准备、Campaign 运营、数据汇总和客户洞察。

所以第一个 AI 岗位可以更具体，例如：

- 每周竞争者研究；
- 产品知识助手；
- 客户常见问题准备；
- Campaign 绩效总结；
- Lead Research；
- 固定运营报告。

一个有用的 AI 岗位需要五件事：

1. 明确责任；
2. 明确信息来源；
3. 可重复工作流；
4. 明确输出；
5. 可衡量的业务改善。

最适合先做的岗位，很多时候并不“酷”。它可能只是每周准备同一份报告、整理研究资料、总结固定信息、帮助员工找企业知识，或者在人工审核之前准备文档。

恰恰因为这种工作很普通，它的价值更容易被看见。

更现实的 AI Workforce 路径是：

```text
一个重复问题
    ↓
一个有用 AI 岗位
    ↓
一个可衡量结果
    ↓
形成重复工作流
    ↓
增加更多专业岗位
    ↓
AI Workforce
```

KING AI 关注的不是 Agent 数量，而是智慧能不能真正进入工作流程。

在购买新的 AI 产品之前，可以先问五个问题：我们要改善哪个重复责任？谁对结果负责？需要哪些信息？AI 应该产出什么？怎么判断它真的帮到了业务？

如果这五个答案已经清楚，一个很好的 Pilot 就已经具备了基础。
